### The Problem with Traditional DEX Farming

Most decentralized exchanges treat incentive programs as a race to the bottom. Whales wash-trade millions in volume with zero fees, extract rewards, and dump on legitimate traders. 

Pacifica solved this by designing a **multi-dimensional points algorithm**:
1. **Trading Volume Weight:** Volume is measured across both taker and maker executions, with fee-weighted tiers.
2. **Open Interest (OI) Loyalty:** Holding positions through high-volatility events earns significantly higher multipliers than sub-second scalping.
3. **Vault & Collateral Staking:** Depositing USDC into user-curated vaults or liquidity pools generates passive point accumulation.

### Mathematical Breakdown: How Points are Calculated

Pacifica calculates user share based on normalized activity scores:

$$\text{User Points} = 10,000,000 \times \left( 0.60 \times \frac{V_u}{V_{total}} + 0.25 \times \frac{OI_u}{OI_{total}} + 0.15 \times \frac{L_u}{L_{total}} \right)$$

* $V_u$: Your fee-adjusted weekly trading volume.
* $OI_u$: Time-weighted open interest held throughout the epoch.
* $L_u$: Net USDC liquidity provided to official Pacifica trading pools and vaults.
