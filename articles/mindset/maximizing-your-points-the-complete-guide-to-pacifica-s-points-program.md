## Part 8: Common Mistakes That Disqualify You


I've seen traders lose thousands of points from simple mistakes. Don't be one of them.


Mistake #1: Wash Trading


The Error: Opening and immediately closing the same position to inflate volume.


The Consequence: Pacifica explicitly prohibits self-trading, sybil attacks, and manipulative activities You'll earn zero points.


The Fix: Every trade must have genuine economic purpose. Open positions, manage them, close them based on strategy — not to game the system.


Mistake #2: Inconsistent Activity


The Error: Trading heavily one week, then disappearing the next.


The Consequence: You lose your consecutive trading bonus (up to 23%)


The Fix: Use the "Daily Touch" method. Even one small trade per day maintains your streak.


Mistake #3: Ignoring the Referral System


The Error: Not creating a referral link after hitting $10,000 volume.


The Consequence: Missing out on 10% passive points from every referee


he Fix: As soon as you hit $10,000 volume, generate your referral link and start sharing strategically.


Mistake #4: Keeping All Capital in Trading


The Error: 100% of funds in active positions, zero in vaults.


**The Consequence: Missing passive points from vault deposits**


The Fix: Use the 60/40 rule. Always have some capital earning passive points.


Mistake #5: Not Tracking Your Points


The Error: Trading blindly without monitoring points accumulation.


**The Consequence: Can't optimize what you don't measure.**


The Fix: Check your points every Thursday after distribution
