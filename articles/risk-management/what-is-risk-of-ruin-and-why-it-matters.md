### What Is Risk of Ruin and Why It Matters

Risk of ruin is the probability that you will lose your entire trading account or reach a point where recovery is mathematically impossible. It is not a vague feeling. It is a precise mathematical calculation based on your win rate, average win, average loss, and position size.

Most traders never calculate this number. They just trade and hope. But hope is not a strategy. If your risk of ruin is 30%, you have a 30% chance of blowing your account. If it is 80%, you are almost certainly going to fail.

![Image 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HM8GbKkWQAAqFgd.jpg?name=large)

The question is not whether you will have losing streaks. You will. The question is whether your account can survive those streaks. Risk of ruin tells you the answer.

### The Mathematical Formula Behind Account Survival

The risk of ruin formula comes from probability theory and gambling mathematics. It was originally developed for casino games but applies directly to trading.

The Formula: Risk of Ruin = (1 - Edge) ^ (Account / Risk per Trade)

**Where:**

* Edge = (Win Rate × Average Win) - (Loss Rate × Average Loss)
* Account = Total account size
* Risk per Trade = Amount risked per trade (usually 1% of account)

Let me break this down with a simple example.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HM8GtqgW4AENdZp.jpg?name=large)

Simplified Version: If you risk 1% per trade and have a positive edge, your risk of ruin decreases exponentially as your account grows. But if you risk 5% or 10% per trade, your risk of ruin increases dramatically.

The key insight: Risk of ruin is not linear. It is exponential. Doubling your position size does not double your risk of ruin. It increases it by 10x or more.

### How to Calculate Your Personal Risk of Ruin

To calculate your risk of ruin, you need data from your trading journal. You cannot estimate these numbers. You must measure them.

Step 1: Calculate Your Win Rate Count your winning trades and divide by total trades. If you have 60 winning trades out of 100 total, your win rate is 60%.

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HM8IjsiWcAAlN00.jpg?name=large)

**Step 2:**

Calculate Your Average Win and Average Loss Add up all your winning trade profits and divide by the number of winning trades. Do the same for losses.

**Step 3:**

Calculate Your Edge Use the formula: Edge = (Win Rate × Average Win) - (Loss Rate × Average Loss)

**Step 4:**

Determine Your Position Size Risk What percentage of your account do you risk per trade? If you risk 1%, use 0.01 in the formula.

**Step 5:**

Apply the Risk of Ruin Formula Plug your numbers into the formula. If you are not comfortable with the math, use an online risk of ruin calculator.

![Image 8](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HM8JWptW8AAwEPJ.jpg?name=large)

### Adjusting Your Trading to Stay Below the Danger Zone

If your risk of ruin is above 20%, you are in the danger zone. You need to adjust your trading immediately. Here are the four levers you can pull.

Lever 1: Reduce Position Size This is the most powerful adjustment. If you reduce position size from 3% to 1%, your risk of ruin drops dramatically. This is the fastest way to improve survival probability.

![Image 9](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HM8KFXwXAAAz3Rt.jpg?name=large)

**Lever 2:**

Improve Your Win Rate This requires better setups, better entries, and better filtering. Focus on your highest probability setups and avoid low-quality trades.

**Lever 3:**

Increase Your Average Win This means letting winners run longer. Use trailing stops instead of fixed take profits. Cut losers quickly but let winners extend.

**Lever 4:**

Reduce Your Average Loss This means tighter stop losses and faster exits when trades go against you. Never move your stop loss further away. Always cut losses at your predefined level.

**The Target:**

Aim for a risk of ruin below 10%. This gives you a 90% probability of long-term survival. It does not guarantee profits, but it ensures you stay in the game long enough for your edge to play out.

* * *

Risk of ruin is not a theoretical concept. It is the mathematical probability that you will blow your account. Every trader has a risk of ruin. The question is whether you know yours.

Calculate your risk of ruin using your actual trading data. If it is above 20%, you are gambling, not trading. Reduce your position size, improve your win rate, increase your average win, or reduce your average loss. Get your risk of ruin below 10%.

Trading is a game of survival. The traders who survive long enough are the ones who let their edge play out over hundreds of trades. If you blow up, your edge does not matter. Calculate your risk of ruin, stay below the danger zone, and let probability work in your favor.
