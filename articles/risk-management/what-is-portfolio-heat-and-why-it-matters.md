### What Is Portfolio Heat and Why It Matters

You risk 1% per trade. You have five open positions. You think your total risk is 5%. But if all five positions are correlated, your actual risk might be much higher. This is portfolio heat, the total risk exposure across all your open positions.

Most traders focus on individual trade risk but ignore portfolio risk. They can survive a single losing trade but get destroyed when five positions move against them simultaneously.

![Image 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HNBT1BCWAAAoBLQ.jpg?name=large)

Portfolio heat is the metric that separates professional traders from amateurs. Professionals know their total exposure. Amateurs only know their individual trade risk.

* * *

### The Mathematical Formula Behind Total Portfolio Risk

Portfolio heat is calculated by summing the risk of all open positions, adjusted for correlation.

Simple Formula (ignoring correlation): Portfolio Heat = Sum of all position risks

**Example:**

* _\_Position 1: 1% risk\__
* _\_Position 2: 1% risk\__
* _\_Position 3: 1.5% risk\__
* _\_Position 4: 0.5% risk\__
* _\_Position 5: 1% risk\__
* _\_Total Portfolio Heat: 5%\__

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HNBUUPjXgAERgOQ.jpg?name=large)

**Advanced Formula**(with correlation adjustment): If positions are correlated, your effective risk is higher. If you have two positions each risking 1% with 0.9 correlation, your effective risk is not 2%, it is closer to 1.9% because they move together.

The correlation-adjusted formula: Effective Risk = √(Σ(Risk_i²) + 2 × Σ(Risk_i × Risk_j × Correlation_ij))

### The Three Danger Zones of Portfolio Heat

Professional traders use specific thresholds to manage portfolio heat. Here are the three zones.

Safe Zone: 0-3% Portfolio Heat Your total risk is low. You can handle multiple losing trades without significant drawdown. This is the zone for conservative traders and beginners.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HNBU_J0WoAARuFo.jpg?name=large)

Caution Zone: 3-6% Portfolio Heat Your total risk is moderate. You can handle a few losing trades, but a correlated crash could hurt. This is acceptable for experienced traders with uncorrelated positions.

Danger Zone: 6%+ Portfolio Heat Your total risk is high. A single market event could trigger multiple stop losses simultaneously. This is how accounts blow up. Professional traders never exceed 6% portfolio heat.

The Math Behind the Thresholds: If your portfolio heat is 6% and you hit a correlated losing streak (all positions stop out), you lose 6% of your account. Recovery requires a 6.4% gain. This is manageable.

If your portfolio heat is 15% and all positions stop out, you lose 15%. Recovery requires a 17.6% gain. This is psychologically difficult and often leads to revenge trading.
