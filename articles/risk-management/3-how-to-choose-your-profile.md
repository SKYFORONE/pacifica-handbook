**What’s a Risk Profile in Trading (and Why You Can’t Ignore It)**

Think of your risk profile as your personal safety net. It shows how much you’re ready to lose per trade and overall. Without it, you’re like a racer with no helmet might be fast, but one crash and it’s game over.

_**In this article, I will explain in simple terms how the pattern works on Pacifica, how to use it correctly and what you should pay attention to**_**.**

![Image 1](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMyOc6bXkAAkEyP.jpg?name=large)

**What Is a Risk Profile Anyway?**

Your risk profile is how you handle risk it’s personal. It reflects:

How much loss you can handle per trade

Your tolerance for drawdowns

Your trading personality: cautious, balanced, or bold?

The goal: make your trading strategy match you, not the other way around.

* * *

**📊 2. Risk Profile Types: Which One Are You?**

**Conservative (Play It Safe**)

→ Risk: 0.5–1% per trade

→ You focus on preserving capital, low leverage, tight stop-losses

→ Ideal for beginners and long-term investors

Example: $10,000 account → 1% = $100 risk per trade

* * *

**Moderate (Balanced Risk-Taker)**

→ Risk: 1–2% per trade

→ You’re okay with taking some risk for better rewards

→ Great for traders with some experience

Example: $10,000 account → 2% = $200 risk

* * *

**Aggressive (High Stakes, High Reward)**

→ Risk: 3–5% or more per trade

→ You go big, use leverage, and trade actively

→ Only for experienced traders with strong nerves

Example: $10,000 account → 5% = $500 risk

* * *

### 🎯 3. How to Choose Your Profile?

* 💰**Money chec**k: What can you afford to lose without losing sleep?
* 🧠**Mental gam**e: Can you stay cool during drawdowns?
* ⏱ **Trading style**:
* Scalping → moderate/aggressive
* Swing trading → balanced
* Investing → conservative

**⚙️ 4. How to Use Your Risk Profile Like a Pro**

**1. Calculate Your Position Size**

Use fixed risk per trade. Formula:

Risk amount / (Stop-loss pips × pip value) = Lot size

**2. Use Stop-Loss & Take-Profit**

Stop-loss: Keeps you from blowing up.

Take-profit: Locks in gains.

👉 Aim for a minimum 1:2 risk/reward ratio.

**3. Diversify**

Never go all-in on one trade. Spread your risk across assets.
