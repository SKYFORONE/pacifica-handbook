**What is a Hammer?**

Hammer is a reversal candlestick that appears after a price drop and signals:

�**� “Buyers have started to regain control”**.

The shape speaks for itself short body on top and long lower shadow. It looks like a hammer with the handle down

_**In this article, I will explain in simple terms how the pattern works on Pacifica, how to use it correctly and what you should pay attention to**_.

![Image 1](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMI4zJqWYAAfkdo.jpg?name=large)

**What does a hammer candle look like?**

A small candle body (green or red not critical).

Long lower shadow at least 2 times longer than the body.

The upper shadow either does not exist at all, or it is very short.

Ideally, it appears at the bottom of a downward movement or at support.

> **This is a signal:**the sellers sold the price downwards at first, but in the end the bulls bought back almost the whole movement.

![Image 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMI5ix4W4AAEfAo.jpg?name=large)

What does the appearance of the hammer mean?

The market fell at first it seemed the bears were strong.

But demand appeared at the bottom the price bounced sharply upwards.

The hammer shows that the downward pressure has eased and a reversal is possible.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMI56ZLWUAAIeA8.png?name=large)

**Where to look for the hammer?**

On a downtrend.

At important support levels.

After a series of bearish candles.

> 👉 The stronger the previous decline the more reliable the signal.

* * *

How to trade the Hammer?

**1️⃣ Entry**

Aggressive: enter at the opening of the next candle after the hammer.

Caution: wait for confirmation the next candle closes above the hammer body.

**2️⃣ Stop Loss**

We put it below the lower shadow of the hammer.

This is logical: if the price returns there, it means that the signal did not work.

**3️⃣ Take Profit**

The first target is the nearest resistance or the maximum of the previous consolidation.

Further you can follow the trend or through a trailing stop.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMI61TqXYAAe0Wc.jpg?name=large)

**✅ Example:**

Price falls from 80 to 70.

Hammer is formed:

Opening: 71

Minimum: 68

Closing: 70.8

On the next candle the price goes above 72 a buy signal.

Stop: under 68

Target: 74–75 and higher.

**✅ Advantages and ❌ Disadvantages**

**✅ Pros:**

Easy to visually recognize.

Works in all markets and timeframes.

Great signal for entering with a short stop.

Cons:

May not work without confirmation.

Gives weak signals in a sideways market.

Requires context: not every hammer is a reversal.

**📌 Tip**s

The ideal hammer is at a key level and on high volume.

Watch what’s nearby: it’s better if the hammer is not alone, but together with an RSI divergence, a level or a pattern.

Don’t confuse it with a shooting star the one on top of the trend and a signal to short.

Hammer candle is the moment when the bears are pushing hard but not winning. And this is the moment when the market gives a chance to go long with a good potential and a short stop.

> **Don’t catch every hammer. Wait for context, confirmation and level then this little pattern will give a big profit.**
