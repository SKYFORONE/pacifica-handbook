> Created by legendary trader Bill Williams, the Accelerator Oscillator (AC) measures the acceleration or deceleration of the current driving market force. Before price changes direction, momentum changes; and before momentum changes, the acceleration of momentum changes.

### The Physics of Price: Why AC Leads Momentum

Think of a moving car:
1. First, the driver eases off the gas pedal (Acceleration drops).
2. Then the speedometer begins to slow down (Awesome Oscillator / Momentum slows).
3. Finally, the car comes to a stop and reverses (Price reverses).

The **Accelerator Oscillator (AC)** is the earliest warning system on the chart. It turns green or red before the Awesome Oscillator (AO) or MACD crosses the zero line.

### Core Signals and Trade Setups

#### Bullish Buy Signal (Above Zero Line)
* Need **two consecutive green bars** above the zero level.
* Entry on the open of the third bar.
* Invalidation stop placed below the lowest low of the setup candles.

#### Bullish Buy Signal (Below Zero Line)
* When below the zero line, downward inertia is still present.
* Need **three consecutive green bars** to confirm reversal acceleration.
* Entry provides an exceptionally early long position with high risk-to-reward.

#### Bearish Sell / Short Signals
* **Above Zero:** Three consecutive red bars required to fight upward inertia.
* **Below Zero:** Two consecutive red bars confirm rapid downward acceleration.

### Filtering False Signals on High-Beta Solana Pairs

On Solana pairs, micro-noise can create single-candle color flips:
* **Rule 1:** Never take an AC signal against the 200-period EMA on the 1-hour chart.
* **Rule 2:** Combine AC with Orderbook Depth on Pacifica. If AC turns green and bid volume thickens on the tape, execution confidence increases tenfold.
