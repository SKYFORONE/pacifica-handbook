## The Command Center: Market Selector and Chart


When you open https://pacifica.fi/, the center of your screen is dominated by the chart. This is your primary analytical tool, powered by professional charting infrastructure.

* Market Selector (Top Left): This dropdown allows you to switch between different perpetual markets (e.g., BTC-PERP, SOL-PERP). It also displays the current 24-hour price change and volume for the selected pair.
* The Chart (Center): Here you can adjust timeframes (from 1-minute to weekly), add technical indicators (like EMA, RSI, or Volume), and draw trendlines. The chart displays the "Mark Price" by default, which is the global index price used to calculate your liquidations fairly, protecting you from single-exchange "scam wicks."

![Illustration 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HN0o7u4WMAA7qe3.jpg)


---


## The Pulse of the Market: Order Book and Recent Trades


To the right of the chart, you will find the real-time market data. This is where you see the actual supply and demand.

* The Order Book: The top half shows "Asks" (red, sell orders waiting to be filled) and "Bids" (green, buy orders waiting to be filled). The number in the middle is the "Spread"  the difference between the highest bid and the lowest ask. A tight spread means high liquidity.
* Recent Trades (The Tape): Below the order book, you see a live, scrolling list of actual market executions. This shows you the exact price, size, and direction (buy or sell) of trades happening in real-time.

![Illustration 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HN0pJdLX0AAvN-S.png)

## The Execution Hub: Order Entry Panel


This is where you take action. Located on the far right (or bottom on mobile), this panel controls how your trade is executed.

* Margin Mode: Toggle between "Cross" (uses your entire wallet balance as collateral) and "Isolated" (restricts risk to a specific amount). Always default to Isolated for directional trades.
* Leverage Slider: Adjust your leverage (e.g., 1x to 50x). Remember: higher leverage shrinks your margin of error.
* Order Type: Choose between "Limit" (set your price), "Market" (instant execution), or advanced types like "PRINT" and "Atomic".
* The Safety Check: Before you click Buy or Sell, look at the "Est. Liq. Price" (Estimated Liquidation Price) and "Available Balance". If the liquidation price is too close to your entry, reduce your leverage or position size.

![Illustration 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HN0pWN_XwAAwmBG.jpg)

## The Truth Teller: Positions and History Tabs


At the bottom of the screen lies the control panel for your active and past trades. This is where you manage risk and review performance.

* Positions: Shows your currently open trades. Here you can see your Entry Price, Mark Price, Unrealized PnL (floating profit/loss), and the crucial "TP/SL" button to set or adjust your Take Profit and Stop Loss.
* Open Orders: Displays your active Limit or PRINT orders that are waiting to be filled. You can cancel them from here.
* Trade History: The ultimate record of truth. This tab shows your Realized PnL, factoring in all trading fees and funding payments. This is your actual, net performance.

## Pro Tip: Customizing Your Workspace


Pacifica is designed for efficiency. You do not have to accept the default layout.

* Use the settings icon on the chart to save your favorite indicator templates.
* Click the "Star" icon next to your most-traded pairs in the market selector to pin them to the top for instant access.
* On desktop, you can often resize the panels (like the Order Book or Order Entry) by clicking and dragging the borders, allowing you to prioritize the data that matters most to your specific strategy.

A professional trader does not fight the interface; they master it. The Pacifica dashboard is built to provide CEX-grade speed, transparency, and control.


By understanding the purpose of the Mark Price chart, the real-time Order Book, the risk controls in the Order Entry panel, and the truth-telling Trade History, you eliminate guesswork.


Take five minutes to explore each section. Set up your workspace. Then, execute your strategy with absolute confidence.
