## What is Swim? (It's Not Traditional Trading)


Most traders on Pacifica use perpetual futures, managing leverage, stop losses, and liquidation prices. Swim is completely different.


**Swim is a prediction game.**


Instead of opening a long or short position and praying the market moves in your direction, Swim challenges you to predict where the price will go and when. You tap a specific zone on a dynamic grid. If the price reaches that zone, you win a payout based on a pre-fixed multiplier.


It turns market analysis into an interactive, gamified experience. But don't be fooled by the simplicity. This is not a casino slot machine. It is a test of your ability to read market volatility and price ranges.

## How the Grid System Works: Zones, Time, and Multipliers


To play Swim effectively, you must understand the three core mechanics that define every round.


1. The Grid (Zones)
The chart is overlaid with a grid of boxes. Each box represents a specific price range. The grid is dynamic, meaning it updates based on current market volatility and price action.


2. The Time Window
Each grid cell is valid for a specific duration. You aren't just predicting "BTC will go up." You are predicting "BTC will touch this specific price zone within the next X minutes/hours."


3. The Multiplier
This is the key to profitability. Each zone has a multiplier attached to it (e.g., 1.5x, 3x, 10x).

* Closer zones (easy targets) have lower multipliers.
* Distant zones (hard targets) have higher multipliers.

Crucially, your multiplier is locked in the moment you tap. Even if the grid shifts and the multiplier for that zone changes later, yours remains fixed.

## Step-by-Step: How to Play Swim on Pacifica


Playing Swim is incredibly simple, but preparation is key.


Step 1: Navigate to SwimFrom the Pacifica dashboard, select "Swim" from the navigation menu. You will see the live chart with the grid overlay.


Step 2: Analyze the GridLook at the current price and the surrounding zones. Ask yourself:

* Is the market ranging (moving sideways)?
* Is it trending (moving up or down)?
* Which zones are realistic targets given the current volatility?

Step 3: Choose Your ZoneTap on a grid cell that you believe the price will touch.

* Conservative Play:  Tap a zone close to the current price (lower multiplier, higher probability).
* Aggressive Play: Tap a distant zone (high multiplier, lower probability).

**Step 4: Enter Your Stake**


Input the amount you want to wager. This is deducted from your main Pacifica balance.


**Step 5: Confirm and Wait**


Once confirmed, your prediction is active. You don't need to manage the trade. There are no stop losses to move, no liquidation prices to watch. You simply wait for the price to hit your zone.

## The Math: Understanding Multipliers and Payouts


Let's look at the numbers. This is where Swim differs from gambling. In a casino, the house edge is fixed. In Swim, your edge comes from your market analysis.


**The Formula:Payout = Stake × Multiplier**


**Example A: The Safe Bet**

* Current BTC Price: $68,000
* You tap a zone at $68,200 (very close).
* Multiplier: 1.2x
* Stake: $100
* Potential Payout: $120 ($20 profit)

**Example B: The Moonshot**

* Current BTC Price: $68,000
* You tap a zone at $72,000 (far away, requires a massive pump).
* Multiplier: 8.0x
* Stake: $100
* Potential Payout: $800 ($700 profit)

The Oracle AdvantageSwim doesn't rely on a single exchange's price, which can be manipulated. It uses an Oracle that aggregates prices from leading CEX and DEX platforms using EWMA (Exponentially Weighted Moving Average). This ensures that the price data triggering your win is fair, accurate, and resistant to manipulation.

## Strategic Play vs. Blind Gambling


Swim is fun, but to be profitable, you must treat it as a strategic tool, not a lottery ticket.


Strategy 1: Volatility Matching
If the market is in a low-volatility consolidation phase, do not bet on distant "moonshot" zones. The price simply won't reach them. Stick to closer zones with lower multipliers but higher hit rates.


Strategy 2: The "Grid Edge" Play
Watch where the grid boundaries are. Often, price will gravitate toward the edges of the current range before breaking out. Tapping zones at the upper or lower limits of the grid during a ranging market can be a high-probability play.


Strategy 3: Bankroll Management
Just like in trading, never risk more than 1-2% of your total capital on a single Swim prediction. The multiplier might be tempting, but a string of losses will drain your account fast if you overbet.


Warning: It's Not Trading
Remember, Swim is a prediction game. You do not own the asset. You cannot hedge your Swim bets with a futures position (unless you manually do so on the main trading interface). Treat it as a separate, high-risk, high-reward component of your Pacifica experience.
