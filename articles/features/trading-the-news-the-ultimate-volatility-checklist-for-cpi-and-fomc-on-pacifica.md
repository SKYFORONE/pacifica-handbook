> Macroeconomic releases like the US Consumer Price Index (CPI) and Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) interest rate decisions trigger violent volatility cascades across crypto markets. Here is the institutional playbook to survive and profit.

### Why Macro Announcements Shred Retail Traders

During the 60 seconds surrounding a CPI print:
1. **Liquidity Evaporation:** Market makers pull resting bid and ask quotes from the orderbook, causing the spread to widen drastically.
2. **Two-Way Whipsaws:** Algorithms hunt stops in both directions — spiking up to liquidate early shorts, then plunging down to liquidate breakout buyers.
3. **Execution Slippage:** Market orders placed during the event fill at the worst possible price on the tape.

---

### The 3-Phase News Execution Framework

#### Phase 1: Pre-Release (15 Minutes Before)
* Cancel all resting non-essential limit orders.
* Reduce total portfolio leverage to **under 2x**.
* Pre-identify key structural support and resistance levels on the 4-hour chart.

#### Phase 2: The Initial Spike (Minutes 0 – 10)
* **DO NOTHING.** Sit on your hands. Never try to trade the first 1-minute candle. Let the algorithms fight and liquidate each other.
* Watch the **World Monitor** for consensus vs. actual numbers and observe whether the initial candle closes with a long rejection wick.

#### Phase 3: The True Trend Trade (Minutes 15 – 45)
* Wait for the initial knee-jerk whipsaw to settle.
* Look for the market to retest the pre-release consolidation range.
* If price reclaims the level with surging volume, enter in the direction of the confirmed macro impulse with a tight stop placed behind the extreme news wick.
