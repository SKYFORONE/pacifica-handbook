## Why Macro News is a Liquidation Trap


When the US CPI data or FOMC rate decision drops, the crypto market does not gradually move. It violently whipsaws. In a single minute, you can see a $1,000 drop followed by a $1,500 recovery.


For traders using high leverage or Cross Margin, this is a death sentence. A momentary wick can trigger your liquidation price before the market even settles into its new direction.


You cannot predict the exact number that will be released, but you can absolutely control how your Pacifica account reacts to the volatility. Preparation is your only edge.


---


## The Pre-News Checklist (T-Minus 1 Hour)


Do not wait until the news drops to check your account. One hour before a major macro event, run this exact checklist on Pacifica:

1. Check Open Positions: Are you currently in a trade that is vulnerable to a sudden move in the opposite direction?
1. Verify Stop Losses: Are your Stop Loss orders hard-coded in the system, or are you relying on a "mental stop"? (Mental stops will get you killed during news).
1. Review Funding Rates: Ensure you are not paying an exorbitant funding rate just to hold a position through the event.
1. Secure Your Connection: Ensure your internet is stable. Pacifica’s Solana-based engine is lightning-fast, but your local connection must keep up.

---


## Adjusting Your Defense: Margin and Leverage


If you choose to hold a position through a high-volatility event, you must fortify your defenses.


**Rule 1: Switch to Isolated Margin**


If you are in Cross Margin, a massive adverse wick can drain your entire wallet balance to keep the position open. Switch to Isolated Margin before the news. This contains the maximum possible loss to the specific amount allocated to that trade.


**Rule 2: Widen the Liquidation Gap**


Look at your "Est. Liq. Price" in the order panel. If the news causes a standard 5-10% volatility spike, your liquidation price must be far beyond that range. If it is too close, reduce your leverage or add more isolated margin to push the liquidation price further away.3


---


## The "No-Trade Zone" Strategy


The smartest trade during a major news event is often no trade at all.


The 15 minutes before and after a major data release is the "No-Trade Zone". Spreads widen, liquidity thins out, and slippage on Market orders becomes severe.


Instead of guessing the direction of the news, professional traders step back. They let the initial algorithmic chaos play out. They wait for the market to absorb the news, establish a new support or resistance level, and then they trade the confirmed direction.


---


## Post-News Execution: Catching the Real Move


Once the initial volatility settles (usually 15-30 minutes after the release), the real opportunity begins.

1. Identify the New Range: Has the news caused a genuine breakout, or was it a fakeout (liquidity grab)?
1. Use Limit Orders: Do not use Market orders to chase the pump or dump. The slippage will eat your edge. Set a Limit order at the newly established support or resistance level.
1. Enable Post-Only: As always, use the Post-Only feature to guarantee you pay the lower Maker fee and do not accidentally suffer slippage.

Macro news events are not a time for heroics. They are a test of your risk management.


By running the pre-news checklist, isolating your margin, respecting the "No-Trade Zone," and waiting for post-news structure to form, you transform a dangerous market event into a calculated opportunity.
