## The Foundation (Security and Capital)


Before you analyze a single chart, your infrastructure must be bulletproof. Based on the official Pacifica Close Beta Guide, your first steps are non-negotiable.

1. Secure Access: Always navigate directly to https://pacifica.fi/. Connect a dedicated Solana wallet (like Phantom or Solflare). Never share your seed phrase, and ignore any unsolicited DMs claiming to be "Pacifica Support".
1. Proper Funding: Deposit USDC on the Solana network for margin, and ensure you maintain a small balance of SOL (e.g., 0.1 SOL) strictly to pay for network gas fees.
1. The 1% Rule: Never risk more than 1% to 2% of your total account balance on a single trade. Calculate your position size based on your Stop Loss distance, not an arbitrary leverage number.

## The Execution Engine (Order Types and Mechanics)


> *Amateurs click "Market Buy" and pray. Professionals control their execution. Pacifica provides CEX-grade order types to eliminate slippage and manage risk.*

1. Default to Limit Orders: Use Limit orders for entries and Take Profits to guarantee your price and pay the lower "Maker" fee.
1. The Post-Only Shield: Always enable the "Post-Only" checkbox on Limit orders. This guarantees your order will only rest on the book. If the market moves and it would fill instantly as a Taker, the system cancels it, protecting you from accidental slippage.
1. Hard-Coded Exits: Never open a position without a predefined Stop Loss. Use the "TP/SL" button in the Positions tab to set it immediately. Treat this as a binding contract; do not move it further away when the trade goes against you.

## The Alpha Generators (Unique Pacifica Features)


> *This is where Pacifica separates itself from legacy DEXs. Once you master basic execution, integrate these unique, documented features into your strategy.*

1. PRINT Orders for Patient Capital: Instead of letting a Limit order sit idle, use a PRINT order. It pays you a daily yield while you wait for your target price, evaluated on a strict 24-hour cycle with an auto-roll mechanism. It turns dead capital into a yield-generating asset.
1. Swim for Volatility Capture: Use the Swim prediction game to capitalize on your market intuition without managing leverage or liquidation. Tap a grid zone, lock in your multiplier, and let the EWMA Oracle determine the payout. It is ideal for ranging markets.
1. Atomic Orders for Complex Strategies: Eliminate "legging risk" (slippage between multiple trades). Use Atomic Orders to open multiple long and short positions simultaneously in a single click. This is essential for instant delta-neutral hedging or basket trading.
