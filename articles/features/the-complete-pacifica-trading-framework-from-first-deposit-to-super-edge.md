### Stage 1: Account Capitalization & Margin Partitioning
* Split your total trading portfolio into two distinct tranches:
  * **Core Treasury (70%):** Kept in USDC earning implicit money market lending yield or allocated to conservative market-making vaults.
  * **Active Tactical Margin (30%):** Deployed for directional swing setups and momentum breakouts.

### Stage 2: Macro Regime Classification
Before placing a single trade, determine the current market regime:
* **Trending Regime (High ADX / Expanding Bollinger Bands):** Trend-following strategies with TEMA ribbons and trailing stops.
* **Ranging Regime (Low ADX / Flat moving averages):** Mean-reversion scalps and PRINT limit orders at range boundaries.

### Stage 3: High-Probability Execution
* Minimum Risk-to-Reward ratio of $2.5 : 1$.
* Confluence of at least 3 indicators (e.g., Support shelf + Bullish Divergence + Volume spike).
* Post-Only limit entry to eliminate taker fees.

### Stage 4: Post-Trade Review & Journaling
* Log trade screenshots into your trading journal.
* Calculate your rolling 30-day Win Rate and Profit Factor ($Gross Profits / Gross Losses$).
