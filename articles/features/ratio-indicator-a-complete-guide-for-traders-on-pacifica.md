### What is the Ratio Indicator?

The Ratio Indicator calculates:

$$\text{Ratio} = \frac{\text{Price of Asset A}}{\text{Price of Asset B}}$$

* Example: **SOL / BTC** or **JUP / SOL**.
* When the ratio rises, Asset A is outperforming Asset B.
* When the ratio falls, Asset B is absorbing market dominance.

On Pacifica, where multi-asset margin and 65+ perpetual orderbooks are interconnected, trading the strongest asset against the weakest asset is the definitive alpha secret.
