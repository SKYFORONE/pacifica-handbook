## World Monitor: Your Real-Time Anomaly Radar


The World Monitor is Pacifica’s dedicated surveillance tool. It continuously scans the entire perpetual futures ecosystem for statistical deviations and unusual market activity.


Instead of guessing where the action is, the World Monitor flags it for you. Key anomalies it helps identify include:

* Sudden, abnormal volume spikes on specific trading pairs.
* Extreme shifts in funding rates (indicating overcrowded long or short positions).
* Large, clustered liquidation events that often signal market capitulation or a squeeze.

This tool transforms raw, noisy market data into high-signal, actionable alerts. It is your radar, telling you exactly where to point your attention.

## The AI Agent: Your 24/7 Quantitative Analyst


If the World Monitor is the radar, the AI Agent is the analyst that helps you interpret the data. Pacifica has integrated AI capabilities that allow users to interact with the platform's data in intuitive ways.

This is not a gimmick. It is a fundamental shift in how traders interact with decentralized finance, turning complex database queries into simple, conversational insights.

## A Simple 3-Step Daily Workflow for Retail Traders


Having powerful tools is useless without a system. Here is a simple, no-code workflow to maximize your edge using Pacifica’s AI tools.


**Step 1: The Morning Scan (World Monitor)**


Before looking at any charts, open the World Monitor. Review the overnight anomalies. Did any of your watchlist pairs experience unusual liquidations or volume spikes? This sets your directional bias for the day.


**Step 2: The Deep Dive (AI Agent)**


Take the top anomaly from the World Monitor and use the AI Agent for context. Ask it to summarize the recent price action, funding rate history, or orderbook depth for that specific pair. This saves you 15 minutes of manual chart analysis.


**Step 3: The Execution (Manual or Automated)**


Based on the AI’s summary, you now have a high-conviction setup. Execute the trade manually using Pacifica’s low-latency interface, ensuring you use Isolated Margin and a hard Stop Loss, as we discussed in previous guides.
