### Step-by-Step Testnet Onboarding

1. Navigate to **[test-app.pacifica.fi/trade/BTC](https://test-app.pacifica.fi/trade/BTC)**.
2. Switch your Solana wallet network to **Solana Devnet / Testnet**.
3. Click the **Faucet** button in the header to claim $10,000 in test-USDC and test-SOL.

### 3 High-Impact Exercises on Testnet
* **Exercise 1 (The Liquidation Stress Test):** Open an intentional 30x position and watch exactly how margin health deteriorates as price approaches the liquidation price. Experiencing this with play money eliminates panic during real trading!
* **Exercise 2 (Slippage Testing):** Compare fill prices between Market and Limit orders on fast-moving 1-minute candles.
* **Exercise 3 (API Script Validation):** If you are building automated bots or using the Pacifica MCP Server, run your code against testnet for 48 consecutive hours before risking capital.
