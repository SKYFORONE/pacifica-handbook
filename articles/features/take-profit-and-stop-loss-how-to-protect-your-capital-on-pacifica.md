> Capital preservation is the single most important rule in professional trading. Anyone can get lucky on a few green candles, but without surgical Take-Profit (TP) and Stop-Loss (SL) execution, one bad day will erase months of progress.

### The Psychology and Math of Stop Losses

A stop loss is not an admission of failure — it is the cost of doing business. It defines the exact price point where your trade thesis is proven invalid:
* If you go long on a support bounce, your thesis is invalid if price breaks and closes below support.
* Keeping an arbitrary stop in your head ('mental stop') is a trap. When price drops, emotion takes over, and mental stops turn into catastrophic hold-and-hope liquidations.

### Setting TP and SL on Pacifica

When submitting an order on [app.pacifica.fi](https://app.pacifica.fi?referral=SKYFOR):
1. Check the **TP / SL** toggle inside the order placement panel.
2. **Stop Loss Configuration:**
   * Enter the trigger price.
   * Choose between **Mark Price** (recommended to avoid brief orderbook wick-hunting) or **Last Price**.
3. **Take Profit Configuration:**
   * Enter target price corresponding to minimum $2:1$ or $3:1$ Risk-to-Reward ratio.
4. **Position-Level Stops:** You can also add or edit TP/SL orders directly from your active **Positions** tab at any time.

### The Trailing Stop Technique
Once a trade moves into $+2R$ profit, move your stop loss to break-even (your entry price). This transforms the trade into a **free roll**, completely eliminating downside portfolio risk while allowing winners to run!
