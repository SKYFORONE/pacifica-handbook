## Why TP/SL is Non-Negotiable


The crypto market is volatile. A 5% move can happen in minutes. If you enter a trade without a predefined exit strategy, you are leaving your capital to the mercy of emotions and market noise.


A Stop Loss (SL) is your insurance policy. It defines the exact price at which your trade thesis is proven wrong, capping your loss to a manageable amount (e.g., 1% of your account).


A Take Profit (TP) is your reward mechanism. It ensures you actually realize your gains before the market inevitably reverses.


On Pacifica, these tools are built directly into the position management interface, making risk management seamless and automatic.


---


## How to Set TP/SL on Pacifica (Step-by-Step)


Pacifica makes it incredibly easy to attach TP and SL orders to your positions. You can do this in two ways:


Method A: When Opening the Trade In the Order Entry panel, before you click "Buy" or "Sell", you will see checkboxes or fields for "Take Profit" and "Stop Loss". Enter your desired prices here. When your main order fills, the TP and SL orders are automatically placed on the book.


**Method B: For an Already Open Position**

1. Scroll down to the "Positions" tab at the bottom of the Pacifica trading interface.
1. Find your active position.
1. Click the "TP/SL" button (or the edit icon) next to that specific position.
1. A modal will appear. Enter your desired Stop Loss price and Take Profit price.
1. Click "Confirm".

## The Psychology of "Moving the Goalposts


**Setting a Stop Loss is easy. Sticking to it is hard.**


When the price approaches your Stop Loss, your brain will scream: "Just give it a little more room! It will bounce back!" If you manually move your Stop Loss further away, you are no longer trading a strategy. You are hoping. And hoping is how accounts get liquidated.


The Rule: Once your TP/SL is set, treat it as a binding contract with yourself. The only acceptable reason to move a Stop Loss is to trail it in your favor (see Section 4), never to give a losing trade more breathing room.


---


## Advanced Move: Trailing Your Stop Loss


Once a trade moves in your favor, your goal shifts from "limiting loss" to "protecting profit". This is where trailing your Stop Loss comes in.


**How to do it on Pacifica:**

1. Your position is in profit.
1. Go to your "Positions" tab and click "TP/SL".
1. Edit your Stop Loss price to a level above your entry price (for a long) or below your entry price (for a short).
1. This guarantees that even if the market violently reverses, you will exit the trade with a profit, not a loss. This is often called "moving to breakeven."

As the trend continues, you can repeatedly adjust this Stop Loss to lock in more and more profit, letting the market take you out only when the trend truly exhausts.
