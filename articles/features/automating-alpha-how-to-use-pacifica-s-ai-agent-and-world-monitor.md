## Integrating AI Tools Into Your Daily Workflow


Having powerful tools is useless if you don't build a system to use them. Here is a simple, 3-step daily workflow to maximize Pacifica’s AI capabilities.


Step 1: Morning Scan (World Monitor) Before you look at any charts, open the World Monitor. Review the overnight anomalies. Did any of your watchlist pairs experience unusual liquidations or volume spikes? This sets your bias for the day.


**Step 2: Deep Dive (AI Agent)**


Take the top 2 anomalies from the World Monitor and query the AI Agent. Ask for context: "What was the funding rate and open interest change for SOL-PERP during the volume spike at 04:00 UTC?" The AI will pull the exact data points, saving you 15 minutes of manual chart clicking.


**Step 3: Execution (Manual or Bot)**


Based on the AI's summary, you either execute the trade manually using Pacifica's low-latency interface, or you adjust the parameters of your Python trading bot (from Article 2) to target these specific conditions.
