### The Top 10 Interface Shortcuts on Pacifica

1. **Instant Percentage Sizing Shortcuts:** Click the $25\%$, $50\%$, or $75\%$ balance buttons in the order panel to size trades instantly without manual calculator typing.
2. **Chart-Based Stop Adjustments:** Drag your active Stop-Loss and Take-Profit lines directly on the interactive TradingView chart to dynamically adjust levels in response to moving market structure.
3. **Toggle Mark Price vs. Last Price:** In the orderbook settings, toggle your chart pricing to **Mark Price** to see the exact reference price used by the liquidation engine.
4. **Post-Only Keyboard Shortcut:** Toggle Post-Only mode before hitting Submit to guarantee zero taker fee execution.
5. **One-Click Position Reversals:** Need to flip bias instantly? The position panel features a fast reverse button that closes your current long and opens an equal-sized short in a single transaction.
6. **Multi-Chart Layouts:** Split your display into 2 or 4 synchronized chart panes to track SOL, BTC, and your target altcoin simultaneously.
7. **Custom Orderbook Grouping:** Adjust tick size aggregation (e.g. from 0.01 to 0.10 or 1.0) to reveal massive institutional liquidity clusters hidden beneath micro-ticks.
8. **Session Delegated Keys:** Enable trusted session keys in your settings to execute multiple orders without signing wallet popups for each individual trade.
9. **Real-Time Margin Health Widget:** Pin the Margin Health gauge to your top toolbar for instant visual confirmation of liquidation risk.
10. **Custom Alert Webhooks:** Set sound and visual alerts at key Fibonacci retracement levels so you never miss a setup while researching.
