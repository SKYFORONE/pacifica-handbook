## Order Entry Hacks for Flawless Execution


The order panel is your cockpit. Mastering it prevents costly "fat finger" mistakes.


**Hack 1: The "Post-Only" Safety Net**


Always check the "Post-Only" box when placing a Limit order. This guarantees your order will only rest on the book as a Maker (lower fee). If the market moves and your order would fill instantly as a Taker, the system automatically cancels it, saving you from unexpected slippage and higher fees.


**Hack 2: The Percentage Sizing Slider**


Instead of manually calculating how many tokens to buy, use the percentage slider (e.g., 25%, 50%, 100%) next to the "Max" button in the order panel. This instantly calculates the exact position size based on your current available margin and selected leverage.


**Hack 3: The Pre-Trade Liquidation Check**


Before you ever click "Buy" or "Sell", force yourself to look at the "Est. Liq. Price" field in the order summary. If that number is uncomfortably close to your entry price, your leverage is too high. Adjust it before submitting.

## Visual Risk Management on the Chart


Managing trades via text fields is slow and prone to error. Use the visual tools Pacifica provides.


**Hack 4: Drag-and-Drop TP/SL**


Once a position is open, you don't need to open a modal to adjust your exits. Simply click and drag your Take Profit or Stop Loss lines directly on the main chart. It is faster, more intuitive, and helps you visualize your risk-reward ratio in real-time.


**Hack 5: Mark Price vs. Last Price Toggle**


Ensure you know which price is triggering your liquidations. Pacifica uses the Mark Price (a global index average) to prevent unfair liquidations from single-exchange wicks. Keep the Mark Price line visible on your chart so you know exactly where your true danger zone is.

## Portfolio and Position Shortcuts


When volatility hits, you need to manage your portfolio in seconds, not minutes.


**Hack 6: The 1-Click "Close Position" Button**


In the "Positions" tab at the bottom of the screen, every active trade has a dedicated "Close" or "Market Close" button. If the market structure breaks and your thesis is invalid, use this to exit immediately at the current market price, bypassing the order entry panel entirely.


**Hack 7: Instant Margin Mode Switching**


Need to hedge a spot portfolio? You can toggle between "Cross" and "Isolated" margin modes directly in the order panel before placing a trade. Use Isolated for directional bets to cap risk, and Cross only when intentionally hedging a broader portfolio.


**Hack 8: Pin Your Favorite Markets**


Don't waste time scrolling through 65+ pairs. Use the "Star" or "Pin" icon next to your most traded pairs (like BTC-PERP or SOL-PERP) in the market selection dropdown to keep them at the top of your list for instant access.

**Hack 9: Monitor the Funding Countdown**


Keep an eye on the funding rate timer at the top of the trading interface. If you are holding a long position and the timer shows 2 minutes remaining with a high positive funding rate, be aware that a fee deduction is imminent. Plan your entries and exits around these intervals.


**Hack 10: Audit the "Trade History" Tab**


At the end of the week, do not just look at your wallet balance. Go to the "Trade History" tab. This shows your Realized PnL, factoring in all trading fees and funding payments. This is the only true measure of your strategy's performance.
