## The Golden Rule of Solana Transfers


Pacifica is built on the Solana blockchain, which means lightning-fast transactions and fractions of a cent in fees. However, this speed requires precision.


Before you move any funds, you must understand the two-asset system of your Solana wallet (like Phantom or Solflare):

1. USDC (The Fuel for Trading): This is your primary collateral on Pacifica. All margin, profits, and losses are calculated in USDC.
1. SOL (The Fuel for the Network): You must always keep a small amount of SOL (e.g., 0.1 to 0.2 SOL) in your wallet. This is used exclusively to pay for network gas fees when you approve transactions, deposit, or withdraw.

Without SOL, your USDC is trapped. Keep this rule in mind at all times.

## Step-by-Step: Depositing USDC to Pacifica


Getting your funds onto Pacifica is straightforward if you follow these exact steps.


Step 1: Connect Your WalletNavigate strictly to the official website: https://app.pacifica.fi - Click "Connect Wallet" and approve the connection in your Solana wallet.


Step 2: Navigate to the Deposit SectionGo to the "Wallet" or "Account" section of the Pacifica dashboard. Select "Deposit".


Step 3: Transfer USDC from Your ExchangeGo to your centralized exchange (like Binance, Coinbase, or Bybit). Initiate a withdrawal of USDC.

* CRITICAL: When asked for the network, you MUST select Solana (SOL).
* Paste your Pacifica-connected wallet address as the destination.

Step 4: Confirm and WaitSolana transactions typically finalize in under 5 seconds. Once the transaction is confirmed on-chain, your USDC will instantly appear in your Pacifica available balance, ready to be used as margin.

## Step-by-Step: Withdrawing Your Profits Safely


You’ve had a successful trading week. It’s time to secure your profits. Withdrawing on Pacifica is just as fast as depositing.


**Step 1: Close All Positions**


Ensure you have no open positions or active pending orders (like Limit or PRINT orders) that are using your USDC as margin. Free up your capital.


**Step 2: Initiate Withdrawal**


In the Pacifica "Wallet" or "Account" section, select "Withdraw". Enter the amount of USDC you wish to transfer back to your personal wallet.


**Step 3: Approve the Transaction**


Your Solana wallet (Phantom/Solflare) will pop up, asking you to sign and pay the network fee. Ensure you have the required SOL balance for gas. Click "Approve".


**Step 4: Funds Received**


Within seconds, the USDC will be back in your personal wallet. From there, you can hold it, move it to cold storage, or send it back to a centralized exchange.

## The 3 Deadly Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)


Even experienced traders make these errors. Protect your capital by avoiding them:


**Mistake 1: Sending USDC via the Wrong Network**


Never send USDC via Ethereum (ERC-20), BSC (BEP-20), or Polygon to your Solana wallet address. The networks are incompatible, and the funds will be permanently lost. Always use the Solana network.


**Mistake 2: Running Out of SOL for Gas**


If your SOL balance hits exactly 0, you will not be able to sign any transactions, including withdrawing your USDC. Always keep a 0.1 SOL buffer.


**Mistake 3: Withdrawing to a Smart Contract Address**


Only withdraw to standard, user-owned Solana wallet addresses (like Phantom or Solflare). Do not attempt to withdraw directly to a centralized exchange deposit address unless you are 100% certain that the exchange supports direct Solana USDC deposits (many still do not).


Pacifica offers one of the most efficient, low-friction trading experiences in decentralized finance, thanks to the speed and low cost of the Solana blockchain.


By respecting the network requirements, keeping a small SOL balance for gas, and always double-checking the withdrawal network, you ensure that your hard-earned profits make it safely back to your wallet.


Trade smart, manage your risk, and withdraw with confidence.
