> Moving funds on and off the exchange should be fast, seamless, and completely secure. Thanks to Solana's sub-second finality, deposits and withdrawals on Pacifica settle in under 2 seconds. Here is the comprehensive operational guide.

### Depositing Funds into Pacifica

1. Connect your authorized Solana wallet at [app.pacifica.fi](https://app.pacifica.fi?referral=SKYFOR).
2. Click the green **Deposit** button in the upper right navigation.
3. Choose your deposit asset:
   * **USDC (Native Solana SPL):** Credited directly as 100% effective USD trading margin.
   * **Spot Assets (SOL, BTC, ETH, LSTs):** Credited to your unified margin account with applicable haircut ratios.
4. Enter the amount, review transaction details, and click **Confirm Deposit**.
5. Approve the signature in your wallet. Your funds appear in your trading balance instantly!

---

### Withdrawing Funds from Pacifica

1. In the account overview panel, click **Withdraw**.
2. Ensure you have zero open margin obligations that would be breached by the withdrawal (check that your remaining Margin Health remains comfortably above 150%).
3. Select the asset and enter your withdrawal amount.
4. Confirm destination address (defaults to your connected Solana wallet address).
5. Click **Confirm Withdrawal**. Pacifica releases the collateral directly to your wallet within a single block.

---

### Crucial Safety Precautions
* **Network Verification:** Always ensure you are depositing native **Solana (SPL)** tokens. Sending Ethereum (ERC-20) or BSC tokens directly to a Solana address will result in irreversible asset loss!
* **Gas Reserve:** Always keep at least $0.05 SOL in your connected wallet to pay for transaction gas when withdrawing.
