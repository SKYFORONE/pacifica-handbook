## Step 1 - Connecting Your Wallet Securely


Your first interaction with Pacifica sets the foundation for your security. Because Pacifica is built on the high-speed Solana blockchain, you will need a Solana-compatible wallet. Phantom and Solflare are the most widely used and trusted options.


**Action**


Always navigate directly to the official URL: https://pacifica.fi/


Click the "Connect Wallet" button in the top right corner.


Select your wallet provider (e.g., Phantom).


Approve the connection request in your wallet pop-up.


Pro Tip: Never click on "Connect Wallet" links from unsolicited DMs on Telegram or X. Always type the URL manually or use a verified bookmark. Pacifica will never ask for your seed phrase.

## Step 2 - Funding Your Account with USDC on Solana


To trade perpetual futures on Pacifica, you need collateral. USDC on the Solana network is the primary and most efficient asset for this, offering near-instant transfers and fractions of a cent in fees.


**Action**

1. Ensure you have a small amount of SOL in your wallet to pay for network transaction fees (gas).
1. Send USDC from your centralized exchange (like Binance or Coinbase) or another wallet directly to your connected Solana wallet address.
1. Ensure you are using the Solana (SOL) network for the transfer, not Ethereum or another chain, to avoid losing funds.
1. Once the USDC arrives in your wallet, it is instantly available to use as margin on Pacifica.

## Step 3 - Navigating the Professional Interface


Pacifica offers a CEX-grade trading interface. Before placing a trade, familiarize yourself with the four main quadrants of the trading screen:

1. The Chart (Center): Your primary tool for technical analysis.
1. The Orderbook (Right): Real-time bid and ask liquidity.
1. The Order Entry Panel (Far Right): Where you select your market, margin mode, leverage, and order type.
1. The Positions/Orders Tabs (Bottom): Where you monitor your open trades, unrealized PnL, and active orders.

Take a minute to click through these areas. The layout is designed for speed and clarity, ensuring you have all the data you need without clutter.


---


## Step 4 - Placing Your First Trade (The Right Way)


This is where discipline matters. Do not just click "Market Buy" with maximum leverage. Follow this professional checklist for your first trade:


Select Your Market: Choose a highly liquid pair like BTC-PERP or SOL-PERP from the market dropdown.


Choose Isolated Margin: This ensures your risk is contained to this specific trade, protecting the rest of your wallet balance.


Set Conservative Leverage: For your first trade, stick to 3x to 5x maximum.


Choose a Limit Order: Instead of a Market order, set a specific price where you want to enter. This guarantees you pay the lower "Maker" fee and avoid slippage.


Check the "Est. Liq. Price": Before confirming, look at the Estimated Liquidation Price. If it is too close to your entry, reduce your leverage or position size.


---


## Step 5 - Monitoring Positions and Managing Risk


Once your order fills, it moves to the "Positions" tab at the bottom of the screen. Your job is not done; it has just begun.


Action:

1. Immediately set a Stop Loss (SL) and Take Profit (TP). You can do this by clicking the "TP/SL" button next to your open position.
1. Monitor your Unrealized PnL (Profit and Loss). This number fluctuates with the market. It only becomes Realized PnL when you close the trade or hit your TP/SL.
1. If the market moves in your favor, consider trailing your stop loss to lock in profits. If it moves against you, trust your pre-defined stop loss and do not move it further away.

Trading on Pacifica is designed to be seamless, fast, and transparent. By following these five steps, you bypass the common beginner mistakes of insecure connections, wrong network deposits, and reckless leverage usage.
