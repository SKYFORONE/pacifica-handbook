## The Problem with "Dead Capital" in Limit Orders


Every trader knows the pain of placing a limit order and watching the market tease you. Price gets close to your target, reverses, and your order sits there for days. Your capital is locked, earning zero yield, while inflation and opportunity cost eat away at your portfolio.


Pacifica solved this with a brilliant, innovative feature: PRINT orders.


Instead of letting your capital sit idle, a PRINT order pays you a daily yield (payout) simply for keeping the order active. It transforms a passive, "dead" limit order into an active, yield-generating asset. This is a game-changer for patient traders who want to be compensated for their time.

## How PRINT Orders Actually Work (The 24-Hour Rule)


To use PRINT effectively, you must understand its core mechanic. It is not a standard market order.


**Here is the exact lifecycle of a PRINT order:**

1. You set a target price and allocate your margin.
1. The 24-Hour Check: Unlike standard limit orders that fill instantly when touched, PRINT orders are evaluated only once every 24 hours.
1. The Payout: If the target price is not reached during that 24-hour window, you receive a daily yield payout, and the order automatically "rolls" to the next 24-hour cycle.
1. Auto-Roll: The order adjusts its target price based on a predefined percentage distance from the new market price, keeping your strategy alive without manual intervention.
1. Execution: If the market price is at or beyond your target at the exact moment of the 24-hour check, the order executes, and your position opens.

## The Hidden Risks: What You Must Know


Pacifica is transparent, but as a disciplined trader, you must understand the trade-offs of the PRINT mechanic. There is no free lunch.


> *Risk 1: The "Wick" Problem
Because PRINT orders only check the price once every 24 hours, a massive, fast wick that hits your target price and reverses within minutes will not trigger your order. You will miss the fill, though you will still get the daily payout. This is a feature, not a bug, but you must be aware of it.*


Risk 2: Cancellation Delay
If you change your mind and want to cancel a PRINT order, it is not always instant. Depending on the cycle, you may have to wait until the end of the current 24-hour period for the cancellation to process and your capital to be fully released.


> *Risk 3: Auto-Roll Drift
When an order auto-rolls, the new target price is calculated based on the current market price. In a strongly trending market, your target price will continuously move away from your original ideal entry point.*

## Pro Strategies for Maximizing PRINT Yield


To get the most out of PRINT, don't use it for every trade. Use it strategically.


Strategy 1: Major Macro Levels
Use PRINT orders only at major weekly or daily support/resistance levels (as we discussed in Article 1). These levels take time to test, making the 24-hour evaluation cycle perfectly aligned with the market's rhythm.


Strategy 2: The "Yield Farming" Range
If you believe a market will chop sideways for a week, place PRINT orders at the top and bottom of the range. You will collect daily yield while waiting for the eventual breakout, turning a boring market into a profitable one.


Strategy 3: Combine with World Monitor
Use Pacifica’s World Monitor to identify when volatility is dropping. PRINT orders perform best in stable, predictable markets where the 24-hour check is less likely to be blindsided by a random, violent spike.
