### Pacifica's Fee Tier Structure

Pacifica rewards liquidity providers with aggressive fee discounts across 8 progressive tiers based on 30-day cumulative trading volume:

| Fee Tier | 30-Day Volume (USD) | Maker Fee | Taker Fee |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Tier 1 (Base)** | $\$0 - \$100K$ | $0.015\%$ | $0.040\%$ |
| **Tier 2** | $\$100K - \$1M$ | $0.012\%$ | $0.035\%$ |
| **Tier 3** | $\$1M - \$5M$ | $0.009\%$ | $0.030\%$ |
| **Tier 4** | $\$5M - \$25M$ | $0.006\%$ | $0.025\%$ |
| **Tier 5+ (VIP / MM)** | $\$25M+$ | $0.000\%$ (Zero!) | $0.020\%$ |

---

### The Massive Financial Difference Between Maker and Taker

Consider a trader doing $\$1,000,000$ in monthly perpetual volume:
* **All Taker (Market Orders):** $\$1,000,000 \times 0.040\% = \mathbf{\$400}$ in fees paid.
* **All Maker (Limit Orders):** $\$1,000,000 \times 0.015\% = \mathbf{\$150}$ in fees paid.
* **Annual Savings:** Over **$\$3,000+$** kept directly in your trading account!

### How to Guarantee Maker Status with Post-Only Orders
1. In the Pacifica order entry box, check the **Post-Only** box.
2. When you place a limit order, Pacifica verifies that it will rest in the book as passive liquidity.
3. If market volatility causes your limit price to cross the spread immediately, the engine safely cancels it instead of charging you an accidental taker fee!
