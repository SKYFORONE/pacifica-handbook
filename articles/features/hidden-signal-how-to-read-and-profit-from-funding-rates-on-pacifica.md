## What is the Funding Rate? (The Anchor Mechanism)


Perpetual futures (perps) do not have an expiration date. So, what keeps the price of a BTC-PERP contract tied to the actual price of Bitcoin? The answer is the Funding Rate.


It is a periodic payment exchanged between long and short traders to keep the perp price anchored to the underlying spot index price.


When the perp price is trading higher than the spot price, the funding rate is positive. This means traders holding long positions must pay a fee to traders holding short positions. This incentivizes traders to sell, pushing the perp price back down to the spot price.


Conversely, when the perp price is lower than the spot price, the funding rate is negative. Shorts pay longs, incentivizing buying and pushing the price back up.


It is a brilliant, self-correcting mechanism. And on Pacifica, this data is completely transparent and updated in real-time.

## How to Read Funding Data on Pacifica


You do not need to calculate this yourself. Pacifica displays all the funding data you need directly on the trading interface.


When you select a perpetual market (like SOL-PERP or BTC-PERP), look at the top of the order panel or the market info bar. You will see the current Funding Rate displayed as a percentage.


More importantly, Pacifica provides a Funding History chart or table. This shows you the historical funding rates over the last 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days.


Always check the countdown timer next to the funding rate. This tells you exactly how many minutes or hours remain until the next funding payment is exchanged.

## Strategy 1: Using Funding as a Trend Confirmation Tool


Funding rates are a powerful sentiment indicator. They tell you exactly how crowded a trade is.


If the funding rate is highly positive (e.g., 0.05% or higher per interval), it means the market is overwhelmingly long. Everyone is betting on the price going up. While this can fuel a rally, it also means the market is vulnerable to a "long squeeze." If the price drops slightly, over-leveraged longs get liquidated, driving the price down further and forcing them to pay massive funding fees.


The Rule: Do not open a new long position when the funding rate is at an extreme historical high. The crowd is already all-in, and the risk-reward is skewed. Wait for the funding rate to cool down or reset to neutral (near 0.01%).

## Strategy 2: The Passive Income Play (Cash and Carry Basics)


You do not have to be a directional trader to profit from funding rates. You can use them to generate delta-neutral yield.


The concept is simple: If the funding rate is consistently positive, you can buy the actual asset (Spot BTC) and simultaneously open a Short position of the exact same size in the perpetual market (BTC-PERP).


Because your long spot and short perp positions are equal, your net market exposure (delta) is zero. You do not care if the price of BTC goes up or down.


However, because you are holding a short perp position during a positive funding environment, you collect the funding fee paid by the longs every single interval. This is a classic, institutional-grade strategy made accessible on Pacifica.

## The Hidden Trap: When Funding Eats Your Profits


Here is the brutal reality that catches many beginners off guard: Funding fees are deducted directly from your available margin.


If you hold a long position for weeks in a market with a high positive funding rate, those periodic deductions will slowly drain your margin. Even if the price of the asset stays exactly the same, your position can eventually be liquidated simply because the funding fees ate up your collateral.


Always factor the funding rate into your holding period. If you are swing trading or holding for days, a high funding rate is a silent killer of profitability. Calculate the estimated daily funding cost before entering the trade.


The Funding Rate is not just a technical detail. It is a direct window into market sentiment, a tool for trend confirmation, and a potential source of passive yield.


Most retail traders ignore it until it is too late. By making it a habit to check the funding rate and its history on Pacifica before every trade, you instantly separate yourself from the crowd.


Respect the mechanism. Use it to confirm your bias, or use it to farm yield. But never, ever ignore it.
