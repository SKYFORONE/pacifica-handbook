## The Golden Rule: Verifying Official Pacifica Links


The most common way traders lose funds is not through a platform hack, but by connecting their wallet to a fake, look-alike website promoted by sponsored search ads or fake social media accounts.


Always bookmark the official, verified links. Do not rely on search engine results alone.


The ONLY official links you should ever use are:

* Main Trading Platform: https://app.pacifica.fi
* Official Documentation: https://docs.pacifica.fi/
* Official X (Twitter): https://x.com/pacifica_fi

## Wallet Hygiene: Your First Line of Defense


Pacifica operates on the Solana blockchain, meaning you interact with it via wallets like Phantom or Solflare. How you manage this wallet dictates your security.


Rule 1: 
Never share your seed phrase or private key. Ever. Pacifica support will NEVER ask for it.


Rule 2: 
Use a dedicated "trading wallet". Do not use the same wallet for trading on Pacifica that holds your long-term, cold-storage savings. If your trading wallet is compromised, your main vault remains safe.


Rule 3: 
Keep a small buffer of SOL. You need SOL to pay for network gas fees. Scammers sometimes send dust (tiny amounts of random tokens) to your wallet. Do not interact with or approve transactions for unknown tokens.

## The "No DMs" Policy: Avoiding Fake Support


As Pacifica grows, scammers will create fake accounts impersonating Pacifica Support or Pacifica Admins on X and Telegram. They will DM you first, claiming there is an issue with your account or offering "exclusive beta access" that requires you to connect your wallet to a malicious site.


**The Pacifica Reality:**


Official Pacifica team members will NEVER direct message you first to ask for wallet connections, seed phrases, or personal information.


If someone DMs you first offering help, it is a scam. Block and report them immediately. For genuine support, always initiate the contact yourself through the official Telegram channel (https://t.me/PacificaTGA) or official X account.

## Smart Transaction Signing on Solana


When you connect your wallet to Pacifica, you will be asked to sign transactions. Modern Solana wallets provide a simulation of what the transaction will do before you approve it.


**Best Practice:**

* When connecting your wallet to https://pacifica.fi/, the request should only ask for "Read" access or a standard connection signature. It should NOT ask to transfer funds.
* When placing a trade, the wallet pop-up will show the estimated network fee and the action (e.g., "Interact with Pacifica Smart Contract").
* If a transaction requests permission to "Transfer All USDC" or interact with an unknown contract, reject it immediately.

---


## Emergency Protocol: What to Do If You Suspect a Compromise


If you accidentally connected your wallet to a suspicious site or signed a malicious transaction, act immediately. Time is your only advantage.

1. Disconnect: Immediately go to your wallet settings and "Disconnect" or "Remove" the Pacifica website (or any suspicious website) from your connected apps.
1. Revoke Permissions: Use a trusted Solana permission revocation tool (like Solana FM or a trusted wallet feature) to revoke any token approvals you may have accidentally granted.
1. Move Funds: If you suspect your private key is compromised, immediately create a brand new wallet and transfer all remaining legitimate funds to it. Do not try to "trade your way out" of a compromised wallet.
1. Report: Alert the community in the official Pacifica Telegram so the team can warn others about the specific phishing link.
