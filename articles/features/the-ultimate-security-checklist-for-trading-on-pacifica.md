### The Pacifica Protocol Security Architecture

Before diving into operational security, understand how Pacifica secures user capital:
1. **Squads Multi-Sig Cold Vault:** The protocol's primary reserves reside in formally verified, multi-signature cold vaults secured by leading ecosystem custodians.
2. **BlockSec Formal Verification:** Smart contract logic is audited by top-tier security firms, with public audit disclosures.
3. **No Direct Asset Custody:** User collateral is held on-chain with isolated programmatic authority — no centralized exchange executive can freeze or rehypothecate user balances!

### The 7-Point Wallet Security Checklist for Traders

1. **Use a Dedicated Trading Wallet:** Never connect your primary cold storage vault (holding your long-term Bitcoin or NFTs) to daily trading frontends. Maintain a separate 'hot' trading wallet with only the capital you intend to deploy.
2. **Verify URL and SSL Certificate:** Always bookmark and verify `app.pacifica.fi`. Phishing clones purchase search ads to mimic trading interfaces.
3. **Review Session Delegated Signatures:** When enabling one-click trading or session keys, verify that the session expiration is set to 24 hours and the spending limit matches your daily volume intentions.
4. **Disconnect Inactive Sessions:** Regularly review connected dApps in Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack settings and revoke stale connection permissions.
5. **Enable Hardware Wallet Confirmation for Withdrawals:** Even if you trade with hot session keys, require a Ledger/Keystone hardware signature for external asset withdrawals.
6. **Protect Your Seed Phrase:** Never type your 12 or 24-word recovery phrase into any website, Telegram bot, or Discord support ticket. No official Pacifica team member will ever ask for your private key.
7. **Monitor Gas Balance:** Keep at least $0.05 SOL in your wallet at all times to prevent transaction failures when closing time-sensitive positions during market crashes.
