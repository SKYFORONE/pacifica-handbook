### What is 'Reading the Tape' on Pacifica?

In perpetual futures, every price tick is the result of market orders consuming resting limit orders:
* **Passive Liquidity:** The Orderbook (resting Limit Buy and Limit Sell orders).
* **Aggressive Liquidity:** The Trade Tape (incoming Market Orders).

When aggressive market buyers hit the ask faster than limit sellers can replenish it, price rises. When aggressive sellers slam the bid, price drops.

### Key Order Flow Patterns to Watch

#### 1. Absorption at Support
* You see aggressive sell prints flooding the Recent Trades feed in rapid succession.
* However, price on the chart refuses to tick lower.
* **Insight:** A large institutional player has placed a massive iceberg or passive limit order, soaking up all retail selling. A violent upside squeeze is imminent.

#### 2. Spoofing and Liquidity Pulling
* Notice a giant bid wall appearing 0.5% below current price.
* As price descends toward it, the bid wall vanishes instantly.
* **Insight:** Fake bids designed to create false confidence. Expect price to flush down.
