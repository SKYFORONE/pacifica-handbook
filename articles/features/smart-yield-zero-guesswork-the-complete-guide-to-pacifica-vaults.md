Before allocating capital into any vault on [app.pacifica.fi](https://app.pacifica.fi?referral=SKYFOR), analyze these four key metrics:
1. **Sharpe Ratio:** Measures risk-adjusted return. A Sharpe ratio above $2.0$ indicates exceptional strategy consistency.

3. **High-Water Mark Status:** Verify that the vault manager operates under an active High-Water Mark contract, guaranteeing they only take performance fees on net-new profits.
4. **Capacity & TVL Velocity:** Monitor vault utilization to ensure you aren't depositing into an over-capacity pool where yield dilution occurs.
