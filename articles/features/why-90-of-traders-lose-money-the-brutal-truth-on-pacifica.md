## The Statistics Nobody Shows You


The trading industry hides uncomfortable numbers. Here is the reality from broker disclosures and platform data:

* 90% of retail traders lose money over 12 months
* 70% lose money in their first year
* 50% quit within 6 months
* Less than 1% achieve consistent profitability over 5 years

![Illustration 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HM3t2dEWsAA7Oed.jpg)


Why do 90% fail? 
Not because trading is impossible. Not because markets are rigged. They fail because human psychology is wired to fail at probabilistic thinking.


**Five Cognitive Biases That Kill Accounts**


Bias One: Loss Aversion Your brain feels losses twice as intensely as gains. This makes you hold losers too long and cut winners too early. You accept small wins but refuse to take small losses.


**Bias Two: Confirmation Bias**


You seek information that confirms your existing beliefs. If you are long, you only read bullish news. You ignore bearish signals until it is too late.


**Bias Three: Overconfidence**


After a few wins, you think you have figured it out. You increase position size, remove stop losses, and take excessive risks. Then one bad trade wipes out weeks of gains.


**Bias Four: Recency Bias**


You overweight recent events. After three winning trades, you feel invincible. After three losing trades, you feel the system is broken and abandon it right before it starts working again.


Bias Five: Revenge
 Trading After a loss, you immediately enter another trade to win back the money. You skip your analysis, ignore your rules, and trade emotionally. This is how accounts blow up.


---


Here is the mathematical reality that destroys traders. If you lose 50% of your account, you need a 100% gain just to break even. If you lose 80%, you need a 400% gain to recover.
This is why risk management is not optional. If you risk 10% per trade, two consecutive losses put you in a 19% drawdown requiring a 23% gain to recover. If you risk 1% per trade, two consecutive losses put you in a 2% drawdown requiring a 2% gain to recover.


The math is brutal but simple. Small losses are recoverable. Large losses are account killers.


---


The solution is not to eliminate emotions. That is impossible. The solution is to build systems that protect you from your own psychology.


Rule One: Never risk more than 1% per trade. This keeps losses small and recoverable.


Rule Two: Write your trading plan before the session. Define your entry, stop loss, and take profit before you enter. Once in the trade, you cannot change the plan.


![Illustration 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HM3uabmXkAAFlTW.png)


Rule Three: 
Keep a trading journal. Record every trade, your emotional state, and whether you followed your rules. Review it weekly. This forces accountability.


Rule Four: 
Take breaks after losses. If you lose two trades in a row, stop trading for the day. Revenge trading is account suicide.


Rule Five: 
Focus on process, not profits. You cannot control whether a trade wins or loses. You can control whether you followed your rules. Measure success by rule-following, not P&L.


---


Ninety percent of traders fail because their brains are wired for survival, not for probabilistic thinking. Loss aversion, confirmation bias, overconfidence, recency bias, and revenge trading destroy accounts.


The solution is not better indicators or secret strategies. The solution is risk management, systematic execution, and psychological awareness. Risk 1% per trade. Follow your plan. Keep a journal. Take breaks after losses. Focus on process over profits.


Trading is not about being right. It is about managing risk and letting probabilities work in your favor over hundreds of trades. The 10% who succeed are not smarter. They are more disciplined.
