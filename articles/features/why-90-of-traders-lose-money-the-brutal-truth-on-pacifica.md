### The 4 Lethal Mistakes that Wipe Out Trading Accounts

#### 1. Excessive Leverage and Asymmetric Losses
Traders see 50x leverage on Pacifica and treat it as standard sizing. At 50x leverage, a mere $2\%$ adverse price fluctuation triggers complete liquidation.
* Lose $50\%$ of your account? You need a $+100\%$ gain just to break even.
* Lose $80\%$ of your account? You need a $+400\%$ gain just to return to zero.

#### 2. The Absence of Hard Stops (Hope Mode)
Unsuccessful traders turn short-term scalps into long-term bag-holding as soon as a trade goes negative. They refuse to accept a small loss, averaging down until margin calls liquidate the entire balance.

#### 3. Over-Trading and Revenge Trading
After taking an emotional loss, retail traders instantly double their position size in the opposite direction to 'make it back'. This emotional state (Tilt) guarantees catastrophic decision-making.

#### 4. Ignoring Market Context and Funding Drag
Holding oversized positions against high funding rates quietly bleeds account equity every single hour, regardless of whether price moves.

### The Pacifica Professional Survival Blueprint

1. **Cap Risk per Trade:** Never risk more than $1\% - 2\%$ of total portfolio equity on any single idea.
2. **Standardize Leverage:** Keep leverage between $2x - 5x$ for swing trading, rarely exceeding $10x$ for intraday setups.
3. **Embrace Unified Margin:** Keep a cushion of unborrowed USDC in your account to absorb liquidity wicks.
4. **Maintain a Journal:** Document setup rationale, entry, exit, and emotional state for every single execution.
