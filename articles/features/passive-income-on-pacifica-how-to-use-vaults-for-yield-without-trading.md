## What Are Pacifica Vaults and How Do They Work?


Pacifica Vaults are automated yield-generating strategies. Instead of manually opening and closing trades, you deposit your capital (usually USDC) into a Vault, and professional strategies work for you 24/7.


**Think of it like this:**

* Active Trading: You steer the car yourself
* Vaults: You hire a professional driver (the strategy) to drive for you

**Vaults on Pacifica can deploy various strategies:**

* Delta-neutral yield farming
* Funding rate arbitrage
* Market-making strategies
* Trend-following algorithms

The beauty? You don't need to understand the complex mechanics. You just deposit, and the Vault does the work.

## Step-by-Step: How to Deposit Into a Vault


Depositing into a Pacifica Vault is straightforward. Follow these exact steps:


Step 1: Navigate to the Vaults SectionFrom the Pacifica dashboard, click on "Vaults" in the main navigation menu.


Step 2: Choose Your VaultBrowse the available Vaults. Pay attention to:

* APY: The projected annual yield
* TVL: Total Value Locked (higher = more trusted)
* Risk Level: Usually marked as Low, Medium, or High
* Strategy Description: What the Vault actually does

![Illustration 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HNMT4BwXkAAyWDv.png)


Step 3: Click "Deposit
Once you've selected a Vault, click the "Deposit" button. A modal window will appear.


Step 4: Enter Your Deposit Amount
Input the amount of USDC (or other supported token) you want to deposit. The interface will show:

* Your share of the Vault
* Estimated daily/monthly returns
* Any deposit fees (if applicable)

Step 5: Confirm the Transaction
Review all details carefully. Click "Confirm Deposit" and approve the transaction in your connected wallet (Phantom, Solflare, etc.).


Step 6: Wait for Confirmation
On Solana, this is fast (usually under 5 seconds). Once confirmed, your deposit is active and earning yield.


Section 3: Understanding Vault Returns: Realistic Expectations


**Here is the truth: APY is not guaranteed.**


> *The APY shown on Pacifica Vaults is a projection based on historical performance and current market conditions. It can change daily.*


**Realistic scenarios:**

* Best case: Market conditions are perfect, APY exceeds projections
* Base case: You earn close to the projected APY
* Worst case: Market volatility or strategy underperformance results in lower returns (or even temporary losses)

Key metric to watch: Historical Performance
Don't just look at the current APY. Check the Vault's performance over the last 30, 60, and 90 days. Consistency is more important than a temporarily high APY.

## The Real Risks: What Can Go Wrong


Risk 1: Smart Contract Risk
While Pacifica is built on Solana with audited contracts, there is always a theoretical risk of bugs or exploits. Never deposit more than you can afford to lose.


Risk 2: Strategy Risk
The Vault's strategy might underperform. For example, a funding rate arbitrage Vault could lose money if funding rates turn negative for an extended period.


Risk 3: Impermanent Loss (if applicable)
Some Vaults that provide liquidity might experience impermanent loss if asset prices diverge significantly.


Risk 4: Withdrawal Delays
Depending on the Vault's strategy, there might be a short delay (hours, not days) when withdrawing to allow the strategy to unwind positions safely.


Risk 5: No FDIC Insurance
This is DeFi. There is no government insurance. Your capital is at risk. Always.

## When to Use Vaults vs. Active Trading


Vaults are not for everyone, and they are not suitable for all your capital. Here is a simple framework:


Use Vaults When:


✅ You want passive income without active management


✅ You have capital you won't need for 30+ days


✅ You are bullish on crypto but don't want to trade actively


✅ You want to diversify beyond just holding tokens


Use Active Trading When:


✅ You have time to monitor markets daily


✅ You want full control over entry/exit points


✅ You are targeting specific price levels


✅ You enjoy the process of trading


Pro Strategy: The Hybrid Approach
Many Pacifica users deploy 60-70% of their capital in Vaults for stable, passive yield, and keep 30-40% for active trading. This gives you the best of both worlds.
