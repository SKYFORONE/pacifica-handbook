### Understanding Pacifica's Vault Architecture

Pacifica Vaults are user-deployed or protocol-curated liquidity pools that execute automated strategies:
1. **Delta-Neutral Market Making Vaults:** Provide bid/ask liquidity across perpetual orderbooks, collecting bid-ask spreads and trading fees while hedging directional delta.
2. **Funding Rate Arbitrage Vaults:** Automatically capture basis spreads between spot and perpetual contracts.
3. **Alpha Manager Vaults:** Community-led strategy pools managed by verified high-performance traders with public on-chain track records.

### Key Investor Protections: High-Water Mark & Risk Limits

* **High-Water Mark (HWM):** Vault managers only earn performance fees if the vault achieves new all-time highs in net asset value. They cannot charge fees on recovered losses!
* **Automated Risk Caps:** Smart contracts enforce strict leverage limits and maximum position sizing, preventing managers from gambling depositor funds.
* **Instant Redemptions:** Withdraw your capital at the conclusion of settlement epochs without lock-up penalties.
