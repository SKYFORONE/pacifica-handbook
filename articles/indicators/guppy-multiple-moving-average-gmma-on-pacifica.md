_\_Guppy Multiple Moving Average (GMMA) is an indicator developed by Darryl Guppy. It helps analyze trends, momentum, and market reversals. The method is based on the use of two groups of moving averages (EMA)\__

![Image 1](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKiMgOzXQAAuiKv.jpg?name=large)

* * *

Short-term group (traders): 3, 5, 8, 10, 12, 15 EMA.

🔹 Long-term group (investors): 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 60 EMA.

💡 How to interpret Guppy MMA:

Wide gap between EMA groups 🔵🔴 → Strong trend.

Crossing EMA groups ❌ → Reversal possible.

Converging EMA 🔄 → Market is losing momentum.

* * *

### How to use GMMA on Pacifica

**Trend detection:**

EMAs are moving apart and up → bullish trend

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKiN0aIW0AAvCKy.jpg?name=large)

EMAs diverge downwards → bearish trend

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKiOayeWYAAUXye.jpg?name=large)

Finding entry points:

When short EMAs cross long ones upwards → buy signal 🟢

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKiOlv3WwAArU8n.jpg?name=large)

When short EMAs fall below long ones → sell signal 🔴.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKiOo6uXsAAKd-f.jpg?name=large)

* * *

Combine with oscillators (**RSI, MACD, Stochastic**) to avoid entering an overbought/oversold market.

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKiPO0PWcAAt_Eg.jpg?name=large)

* * *

### GMMA Trading Strategies on Pacifica

**Trend Strategy:**

Enter long when short EMAs diverge upwards and longs also rise.

![Image 8](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKiPwWtWcAExkzw.jpg?name=large)

* * *

### Example of use on Pacifica

Let’s say you’re trading BTC, and the short EMAs diverge sharply upwards, while the long ones also start to grow. This is a signal for a strong uptrend, and you can consider entering a long. If the EMAs start to converge, you should be more careful a reversal is possible.

🚀 GMMA tips for Pacifica

**Don’t rush** wait for a clear signal before entering.

**Use GMMA with other indicators** volume, oscillators, trend lines.

**Watch for narrowing EMAs** this is an early sign of the end of a trend.

> Guppy Multiple Moving Average is a great tool for assessing trends and entering trades. Use it on Pacifica to catch strong moves and minimize risks!
