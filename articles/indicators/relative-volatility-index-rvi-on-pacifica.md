What is the Relative Volatility Index (RVI)?

The Relative Volatility Index (RVI) is an indicator that measures market volatility and helps determine the strength of a trend.

* * *

💡 The main idea of ​​RVI:

If RVI is above 50 → the trend is bullish, the market is active.

If RVI is below 50 → the trend is bearish, the market is weakening.

👉 Difference from RSI:

RSI analyzes the strength of the price.

RVI analyzes the strength of volatility (price variability).

### How is RVI calculated?

RVI is based on the standard deviation of the price over a certain period.

The indicator fluctuates between 0 and 100, where:

0–30 → low volatility, the market is fading.

70–100 → high volatility, a strong trend is possible.

**Standard settings:**

Period: 14 (can be adjusted to suit your trading style).

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLVQblYXIAAuHtV.jpg?name=large)

### How to interpret RVI signals?

1. Level 50 — direction indicator

If RVI is above 50 → the market is growing, look for purchases.

If RVI is below 50 → the market is weakening, look for sales.

> This signal is useful for filtering the trend.

1. Overbought and oversold

If RVI is above 70 → the market is overheated, a downward reversal is possible.

If RVI is below 30 → the market is oversold, an upward rebound is possible.

> Works like RSI, but with an emphasis on volatility!

1. Divergence

If the price is rising, and RVI is falling → the trend is weak, a downward reversal is possible.

If the price is falling, and RVI is rising → the market is preparing for growth.

> Divergence is a powerful signal to enter a trade!

### How to use RVI in trading on Pacifica?

📌 1. Confirmation of the trend

If RVI is above 50 and the price is above the moving average, then the trend is strong.

✅ Example of a trade:

RVI is above 50.

Price is above 50 EMA.

We open a long, stop loss under the nearest support.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLVRmAmXUAAQ8lK.jpg?name=large)

**📌 We exit the trade when RVI falls below 50**.

* * *

**📌 2. Search for reversals by divergenc**e

If the price makes a new high, and RVI decreases, we wait for a reversal.

✅ Example of a trade:

The price updates the high, but RVI falls.

RSI shows overbought.

Open a short, stop loss above the high of the candle.

Pros and cons of RVI

Pros:

✔ Suitable for trend and counter-trend trading.

✔ Shows the strength of volatility, not just price.

✔ Can be used to filter trades.

Cons:

✖ May lag on sharp movements.

✖ Gives false signals in a sideways trend.

> Use RVI on H1 and above for more accurate signals!

**Relative Volatility Index (RVI) is a powerful indicator for assessing trend strength and volatility.**

✔ If RVI is above 50 → look for purchases.

✔ If RVI is below 50 → the trend is weak, look for sales.

✔ Divergence of RVI and price is a strong reversal signal.

> Combine RVI with RSI and MACD for better accuracy!
