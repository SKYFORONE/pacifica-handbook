_\_Balance of Power (BOP) is an indicator that measures the ratio between the strength of buyers and sellers. It helps to determine who dominates the market and in which direction the price may move.\__

### How Balance of Power works:

BOP calculates how close the closing price is to the high or low for the selected period. The indicator value ranges from -1 to +1:

+1 — Buyers are in complete control of the market (bullish signal).

-1 — Sellers dominate (bearish signal).

0 — Balance of power between buyers and sellers.

How to use Balance of Power on Pacifica

**Trend Determination:**

If BOP is above 0, the market is dominated by buyers, the trend is upward.

If BOP is below 0, sellers dominateб the trend is downward.

**Searching for reversal points:**

Abrupt changes in BOP can indicate a trend reversal.

If BOP goes from a negative zone to a positive zone, it is a buy signal.

**Confirmation of trend strength:**

A long BOP in the positive zone confirms a strong uptrend.

### Example of use on Pacifica

Suppose the price of an asset on the Pacifica is rising and the BOP goes from _**-0.3 to +0.6.**_ This is a strong signal that buyers have taken control and a long position can be opened.

![Image 9](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKScJoKWMAA-wlf.jpg?name=large)

* * *

If after a long BOP is in the positive zone, the indicator starts to decline and crosses 0, it is a signal to take profits or open a short.

**🚀 Tips for effective work with BO**P

**Watch out for extreme values:**

If BOP reaches +1 or -1, a quick reversal is possible.

Use BOP with volume: Sharp changes in BOP with high volume are more reliable signals.

Filter out false signals: Don’t rely on BOP aloneб combine it with RSI, MACD or Keltner Channel.

Risk Management: Set a stop loss behind the nearest support or resistance level to protect capital.
