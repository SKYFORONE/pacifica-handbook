_\_Majority Rule Indicator (MRI) is an indicator that helps to determine the dominant market direction based on signals from several technical indicators. Its purpose is to confirm the trend and reduce the probability of false signals.\__

### How Majority Rule Indicator works:

MRI combines several popular indicators (e.g. Moving Averages, RSI, MACD) and analyzes which signal prevails. If the majority of them point in the same direction, the indicator gives a corresponding signal:

**🟢Buy** when most indicators confirm an uptrend.

**🔴Sell** when the majority of indicators indicate a downtrend.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKSPsZNWAAAj5Yo.jpg?name=large)

If MRI is signaling Sell, it is a reason to consider selling and the downtrend.

### Example of use on Pacifica

Imagine that on the Pacifica, the price of an asset is fluctuating around an important resistance level. When the Majority Rule Indicator shows Buy, it is a buy signal, especially if confirmed by rising volumes and other indicators.

If the price continues to rise and the signal remains Buy, hold your position. If the signal changes to Sell, take profit and consider opening a short.

**🚀 Tips for working effectively with MRI**s

Don’t ignore confirmation: Use**MRI** together with other indicators (e.g. **Bollinger Bands or MACD**) to minimize false signals.

Customize parameters: In some systems, **MRI** can be customized by choosing which indicators to consider and their periods.

Watch for volatility: During flat conditions,**MRI** can give false signals. During periods of strong trend its accuracy is higher.

Risk Management: Place a stop loss at the nearest support or resistance levels to minimize losses.
