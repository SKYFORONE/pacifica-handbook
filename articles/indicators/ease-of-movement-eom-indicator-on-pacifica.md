### What is Ease of Movement?

Ease of Movement is a technical indicator that shows how easily the price moves up or down on low volumes.

**In simple terms:**

If the price rises easily (on low volumes), it is a good signal for bulls.

If the price falls easily (also on low volumes), it is a sign of market weakness.

EOM helps traders assess whether there is serious volume behind the price movement or if it is just a random jerk without support.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKjEnhNXEAAD0bk.jpg?name=large)

* * *

### How is Ease of Movement calculated?

The indicator is calculated using a formula, but don’t worry, we’ll break it down simply.

Average price = (Maximum + Minimum) / 2

Range = Difference between maximum and minimum

Volume is taken in millions or other large units

How to understand this?

If EOM is high and positive → Price rises easily, trend is up.

If EOM is negative and low → Price falls easily, trend is down.

If EOM is near zero → Market is balanced, no special movement.

How to interpret Ease of Movement?

1️⃣ Rising on low volumes is a bullish signal

If the price is moving up and the volume remains low, it means the market is ready to continue rising.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKjFJAsXwAAkZgP.jpg?name=large)

2️⃣ Falling on low volumes is a bearish signal

If the price is falling without strong volume, it is a sign of weakness. Perhaps the price is simply “sliding down” without pressure from large sellers.

3️⃣ Sharp upswing on low volume be careful!

If EOM is rising sharply, but volumes are low it may be a false breakout. It is better to wait for confirmation.

**How to use Ease of Movement in trading?**

📌 1. In conjunction with the trend

If the trend is up and EOM remains positive, the trend is reliable.

If the trend is up but EOM is falling, the trend is losing strength.

In combination with other indicators

It is better to use EOM together with moving averages (**SMA, EMA**), **RSI** or MACD.

Example:

EOM shows ease of growth → look at RSI. If it is above 50, this confirms the strength of the bulls.

EOM is falling, but the price is not changing yet → we can expect a downward reversal.

* * *

Ease of Movement is a simple but effective indicator that shows whether the price moves up or down easily. It helps to find moments when the market is ready to continue the trend or, on the contrary, is losing strength.

**📌 Key findings**:

✅ If EOM is rising — a bullish signal.

❌ If EOM is falling — a bearish signal.

📉 If EOM is around 0 — the market is in balance.
