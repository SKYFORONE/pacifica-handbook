> Accumulative Swing Index (ASI) is an indicator developed by Welles Wilder that helps analyze trend direction and strength, confirm breakdowns and identify reversals. It is based on opening, closing, highs and lows, which allows for a more accurate assessment of market movements.

### How Accumulative Swing Index (ASI) works

ASI accumulates Swing Index (SI) values and displays them in the form of a line that reflects the general trend. The main signals are formed based on the interaction of the ASI line with the price chart.

**Key operating principles:**

A positive ASI value confirms an uptrend.

A negative ASI value confirms a downtrend.

The crossing of an important ASI level may signal a breakdown and the beginning of a new trend.

### How to use Accumulative Swing Index on Pacifica

**Trend confirmation**:

If the ASI is rising and in the positive zone, this is a bullish trend signal.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNOHGbWQAEqaYB.jpg?name=large)

If the ASI declines and stays in the negative zone, it is a bearish trend.

Long (Buy): When the ASI breaks the previous high, it is a signal to enter a long position.

### Practical example on Pacifica

Imagine that on the Pacifica the ASI line breaks the previous local maximum, this is a buy signal.

If the ASI line begins to decline and crosses an important minimum, it is a signal to exit the position and the possibility of opening a short trade.

**Tips for trading with Accumulative Swing Index**

**Adjust the parameters to the market**: The standard parameter is a period of 14, but for more accurate signals on Pacifica try 20 or 30.

**Look for divergences**: If the price makes a new high and the ASI does not confirm this movement, a reversal is possible.

**Combine with other indicators**: Use MACD to confirm momentum and Bollinger Bands to analyze volatility.

**Risk Management**: Always set a stop loss behind the nearest extremes to minimize risks.

Accumulative Swing Index is a reliable tool for confirming trends and identifying breakdowns. Use it in combination with other indicators on Pacifica to improve the accuracy of entries and exits!
