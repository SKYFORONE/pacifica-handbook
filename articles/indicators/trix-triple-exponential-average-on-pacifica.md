_\_TRIX (Triple Exponential Average) is an indicator that shows the rate of change of the triple exponential moving average. It helps to identify the trend direction and filter market noise, providing more accurate signals for entering and exiting positions.\__

### How the TRIX Indicator works

TRIX is based on three times smoothing of the exponential moving average (EMA), which eliminates minor fluctuations and focuses on the underlying trend. The indicator fluctuates around zero:

🟢 Above 0 Uptrend, a buy signal.

🔴 Below 0 Downtrend, sell signal.

* * *

### How to use TRIX Indicator on Pacifica

**Trend Determination:**

If TRIX is above 0, it is a bullish trend signal.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKSmufvXQAAYAGT.jpg?name=large)

If TRIX is below 0, the market is in a bearish trend.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKSniAAWgAAEu0w.jpg?name=large)

Zero line crossing:

Long: When TRIX crosses the zero line from bottom to top, it is a buy signal.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKSoGDiWMAAYd2l.jpg?name=large)

Short: If TRIX crosses the zero line from top to bottom, it is a sell signal.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKSoa6rWwAAOD2p.jpg?name=large)

* * *

### Divergences

If the price makes a new high and the TRIX does not confirm it, a downward reversal is likely.

If the price makes a new low and the TRIX remains above, an upward reversal is likely.

### Trading Strategies with TRIX on Pacifica

Zero Line Crossing Strategy:

Buy when the TRIX crosses 0 from bottom to top

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKSpMZVW8AAp8mg.jpg?name=large)

Sell if TRIX crosses 0 from top to bottom.

![Image 8](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKSowz8W4AAPwTH.jpg?name=large)

**Combine with other indicators:**

Use TRIX with moving averages (EMAs) to filter out false signals.

### Example of use on Pacifica

Suppose the price of an asset on Pacifica is moving upwards and TRIX crosses the zero line from the bottom to the top. This is a buy signal.

If later TRIX starts to decline and crosses 0 from the top to the bottom, it is a signal to take profits or open a short.

**🚀 Tips for working effectively with TRI**X

Adjust the parameters: Experiment with the TRIX period length (default 14) shorter periods react faster but give more false signals.

Filter out false signals: Confirm TRIX signals with other indicators (e.g. MACD or Bollinger Bands).

**Use divergences:** Don’t ignore divergences between TRIX and price they often predict reversals.

**Risk Management**: Always set a stop loss at support and resistance levels.
