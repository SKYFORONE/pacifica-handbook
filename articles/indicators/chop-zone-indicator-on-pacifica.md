### How Chop Zone Indicator works

The indicator is based on Choppiness Index values and displays them in the form of colored bars that signal the market state:

Green bars the market is in a trend (the brighter, the stronger the movement).

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIEmf_WAAA84Ht.jpg?name=large)

The market is flat, high probability of consolidation.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIE94GWMAAxfeD.png?name=large)

* * *

### Key operating principles:

If green bars prevail, it is a signal of directional movement.

If the indicator shows red bars, the market is in a sideways trend it is better to avoid entering a position.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIFVqZXcAAwdD-.jpg?name=large)

* * *

### How to use Chop Zone Indicator on Pacifica

**Green bars signaling the beginning or continuation of a strong trend**

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIF9aCX0AAFwzc.jpg?name=large)

**Red bars** indicate consolidation or uncertainty.

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIGYDLXwAAcWc1.jpg?name=large)

**Finding entry points:**

Long (buy): Wait for the red bars to change to green, this signals the start of an uptrend.

![Image 8](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIG8EnXYAAxzFN.jpg?name=large)

**Short (sell): If green bands downward appear after a period of consolidation, you can open a short position.**

### Practical example on Pacifica

Let’s say that several candles in a row are displayed with red bars on the **Pacifica**, which indicates a flat market. As soon as the bright green bars appear, it is a buy signal as the market starts to move up.

f the indicator changes to red again, it is a sign of profit taking.

Don’t trade in the red zone: When the market is flat, false signals are more likely.

**Use in conjunction with trend indicators**: For example, Moving Average or MACD can help confirm a trend.

**Customize the parameters**: The default period is 14, but 21 works well on Pacifica for more reliable signals.

**Risk Management**: Place a stop loss behind the nearest support and resistance levels.

The 👉 Chop Zone Indicator is great for filtering out market noise and helps distinguish strong trends from flat. Use it in combination with other indicators on Pacifica to find the best entry and exit points!
