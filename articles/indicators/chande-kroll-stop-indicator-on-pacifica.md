### How Chande Kroll Stop works:

The indicator calculates the volatility of an asset based on the average true range (ATR).

**It creates two lines**: an upper line (stop for a short) and a lower line (stop for a long).

When the price breaks one of the lines, it signals a possible reversal.

### How to use Chande Kroll Stop on Pacifica

**Stop Loss Setting:**

In a short, put a stop loss just above the top line.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKczcxBW8AAVNO4.jpg?name=large)

When longing, place your stop loss just below the lower line of the indicator.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKcz7hxWYAAeDfF.jpg?name=large)

**Trend Determination:**

If the price is above the lower line, the trend is bullish.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKc0hXuWIAANfNg.jpg?name=large)

* * *

### Trading Strategies with Chande Kroll Stop on Pacifica

**Buy if the price is above the lower line and the trend is upward.**

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKc1QJhWcAATrYW.jpg?name=large)

Sell if the price is below the upper line and the trend is downward.

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKc15g0XEAAfYsT.jpg?name=large)

### Example of use on Pacifica

Imagine that you have opened a long position and the price is rising steadily. Chande Kroll Stop pulls the lower line up, showing the level below which you should place a stop loss. If the price breaks this level, you should lock in profit or exit the trade.

🚀 Tips for effective work with Chande Kroll Stop

**Don’t use the indicator alone**: It is better to combine it with trend instruments (e.g. Moving Averages).

**Watch for changes in the lines**: Sharp spikes can herald a trend reversal.

**Adjust the parameters to your trading style**: The higher the period, the less frequent the signals, but they are more accurate.
