_\_Directional Movement Indicator (DMI) is an indicator that helps determine the direction of a trend and its strength. It was developed by Welles Wilder and consists of three key lines:\__

* * *

+DI (positive movement) — shows the strength of bulls (buyers).

🔹 -DI (negative movement) — shows the strength of bears (sellers).

🔹 ADX (average direction of movement) — indicates the strength of the trend.

How to interpret DMI:

+DI above -DI → bullish trend 📈.

-DI above +DI → bearish trend 📉.

ADX above 25 → strong trend, can be traded.

ADX below 20 → weak trend or sideways.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKi4Bf7XEAEBXdj.png?name=large)

### How to use DMI on Pacifica

**Determining the trend direction:**

If +DI is higher than -DI and ADX is rising, then the trend is up.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKi4sNZW8AEhINc.jpg?name=large)

If -DI is higher than +DI and ADX is rising, then the trend is downward.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKi5EHeWkAA8Fwa.jpg?name=large)

Finding entry points:

When +DI crosses -DI upwards, it is a buy signal.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKi5ONlXsAAIgof.jpg?name=large)

When -DI crosses +DI upwards, it is a sell signal.

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKi5T4KW4AAvd_D.jpg?name=large)

Filtering false signals:

If ADX is below 20, it is better to refrain from trading.

![Image 8](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKi5aQ_W8AA7SaD.jpg?name=large)

The higher the ADX, the stronger the trend and the more reliable the signal.

### DMI Trading Strategies on Pacifica

**Trend Following Strategy:**

Wait for ADX to be above 25.

If +DI crosses -DI upwards, open a long 🟢

If -DI crosses +DI upwards, open a short 🔴

Trade exit strategy:

If ADX starts to decline, the trend is weakening.

At a new intersection of +DI and -DI, ​​you can take profit.

### Example of use on Pacifica

Let’s say you’re trading BTC/USDT and you see that +DI has crossed -DI upwards and ADX is rising. This confirms the start of a bullish trend and you can enter a long. If ADX starts to decline, you should think about exiting the trade.

DMI tips forPacifica

Don’t trade in a sideways market it’s better to use DMI when ADX is above 25.

Combine with other indicators, such as moving averages.

Watch for +DI and -DI crossings these are key entry signals.

👉 DMI is a great tool for identifying a trend. Use it on Pacifica to find entry points and avoid a flat market!
