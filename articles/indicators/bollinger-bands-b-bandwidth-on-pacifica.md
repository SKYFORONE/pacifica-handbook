_\_Bollinger Bands %B (or simply %B) is a technical indicator that shows where the price is relative to the Bollinger Bands. Bollinger Bands are three lines:\__

_**Middle line (moving averag**_ _\_e)\__

_\_Upper boundary (average + standard deviation)\__

_\_Lower boundary (average — standard deviation)\__

_\_%B shows how close the price is to the upper or lower boundary of these bands.\__

How Bollinger Bands %B is Calculated

This indicator changes from 0 to 1 (sometimes goes beyond these limits):

_\_0 — price at the lower limit\__

_\_1 — price at the upper limit\__

_\_>1 — price above the upper band (overbought)\__

_\_<0 — price below the lower band (oversold)\__

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKi8iG_W4AAIax3.jpg?name=large)

**How to interpret the %B indicator**

If %B is close to 1 (or higher)

The price is at the upper Bollinger band

This may indicate overbought

A pullback is possible

**If %B is close to 0 (or lower)**

Price is at the lower Bollinger band

This may mean oversold

Possible bounce up

**If %B is around 0.5**

Price is in the middle of the bands

### How to use %B in trading

1️⃣**Finding overbought and oversold**

If %B > 1, the asset may be overheated and a pullback is possible.

If %B < 0, the asset may be too cheap and growth is possible.

2️⃣**Confirming a breakout**

If the price goes beyond the bands and %B stays there, it is a signal of a strong trend.

If %B returns to the range, the breakout may be false.

3️⃣**Divergences**

If the price updates the maximum, and %B is below the previous peak a signal to reverse down.

If the price updates the minimum, and %B is above the previous minimum a signal to reverse up.

* * *

Examples of trading with %B

**🟢 Buy**: %B < 0, price touches the lower band, then bounces.

**🔴 Se**ll: %B > 1, price touches the upper band and starts to decline.

Filtering false signals: Use %B with other indicators (RSI, MACD).

Bollinger Bands %B is a useful tool for understanding where the price is within the Bollinger Bands. It helps to find entry and exit points, but works best when combined with other indicators.
