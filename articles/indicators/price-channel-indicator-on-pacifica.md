**📌 What is Price Channel**?

Price Channel is an indicator that draws a channel on the chart from two lines: upper and lower.

These lines show:

The highest price for the selected period (upper line).

The lowest price for the same period (lower line).

👉 Simply put, Price Channel shows the limits of price movement over a period of time.

**🔍 How does Price Channel work**?

The indicator takes, for example, the last 20 candles and:

Finds the maximum of the closing prices or the maximum at all.

Finds the minimum for the same 20 candles.

Draws horizontal lines through them.

📈 You get a kind of corridor in which the price walks.

The upper line is the resistance level.

The lower line is the support level.

**📊 How to read Price Channel signals**?

Price breaks through the upper boundary → signal for continued growth (or the beginning of a bullish trend).

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLf3fi2a4AAy9vt.jpg?name=large)

Price breaks through the lower boundary → signal to continue falling (or the beginning of a bearish trend).

Price moves inside the channel → the market is flat, you can work from the borders.

⚡️ Important: the longer the price has not broken through the channel, the stronger the movement after the breakdown can be.

### 💡 How to use Price Channel in trading on Pacifica?

1️⃣ Channel Breakout Strategy

When the price breaks the upper or lower line:

Enter in the direction of the breakdown.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLf4DFxXcAAokMy.jpg?name=large)

1️⃣Put stop loss just behind the opposite border of the channel.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLf4YXPWcAA-0qI.jpg?name=large)

Take Profit can be set at a fixed size or trailing stop.

* * *

✅ Example: Price broke through the upper boundary of the channel upwards → buy → hold until signs of reversal.

2️⃣ Bounce from Borders Strategy

If the market is flat:

Sell from the upper boundary.

Buy from the lower boundary.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLf5jemWAAATK1t.jpg?name=large)

> **Stop loss**should be placed a little behind the channel lines.

* * *

📌 Works better on calm markets or at night, when movements are sluggish.

**3️⃣ False Breakdown Filtering**

Price Channel combines well with:

Volumes: a breakout on high volumes is real.

RSI or Stochastic: if the breakout and oscillator confirm, the chance is higher.

**✅ Advantages and ❌ Disadvantages**

**✅ Advantages:**

Clear structure: you can see where support is, where resistance is.

Versatility: you can trade both breakouts and rebounds.

Easy to understand, even for beginners.

**❌ Disadvantages:**

There are often false breakouts without confirmation.

Signals sometimes lag in sideways conditions.

It does not show the strength of the trend by itself it is better to supplement with other indicators.

Price Channel is one of the simplest and easiest ways to see the market structure.

It is ideal for finding breakdowns and trading from levels, it helps to understand when the market is flat and when it is ready for a breakout.

**📌 Tip: always check the breakout with volumes or oscillators. Don’t jump into a trade just because one candle went out of the channel**!
