True Strength Index (TSI) is an oscillator that helps to identify trend strength and pivot points. The indicator is based on double exponential smoothing, which allows it to filter market noise and give more accurate signals.

### How True Strength Index (TSI) works

The indicator consists of two key elements:

Main Line (TSI Line) reflects the strength and direction of the trend.

The Signal Line is a smoothed version of the main line used to confirm signals.

![Image 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKDKYItW4AA1rF7.png?name=large)

### Key operating principles:

When TSI crosses the signal line from bottom to top it is a buy signal.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKDKyKQWIAATHwk.png?name=large)

**When TSI crosses the signal line from top to bottom, it is a sell signal.**

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKDLOIqXMAA83Yt.jpg?name=large)

Values above +25 indicate overbought, below -25 indicates oversold.

* * *

Long (buy): When the TSI crosses the signal line from bottom to top in the area below 0.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKDLjFOWcAAUI26.jpg?name=large)

Short (sell): When the TSI crosses the signal line from top to bottom in the zone above 0.

* * *

### Filtering out false signals:

Avoid entering if the crossover occurs near the zero mark this is an area of uncertainty.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKDMEN_XMAAzNwl.jpg?name=large)

Use TSI on H1 and above to avoid market noise on smaller timeframes.

Exit the trade:

Close the position when a reverse crossover occurs or the TSI reaches extreme values +25/ -25

* * *

### Practical example on Pacifica

Suppose on the **Pacifica**, TSI crosses the signal line from bottom to top and both lines are below 0. This is a buy signal. Hold the position until the TSI crosses back or reaches the overbought area.

If the TSI approaches +25 and starts to decline, this is a signal to take profits.

**🚀 Tips for trading with True Strength Inde**x

Combine with other indicators: Use Moving Average or RSI to confirm signals.

Watch the extremes: The +25 and -25 levels help to identify potential reversals.

**Risk Management:** Place a stop loss outside the recent local lows/maximums to protect against false signals.

👉 True Strength Index is a versatile indicator that is suitable for both short-term and long-term trading. Use it on Pacifica to find reliable signals and manage your positions wisely!
