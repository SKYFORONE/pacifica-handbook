_\_The Detrended Price Oscillator (DPO) is an indicator that helps remove the long-term trend from price to more easily identify cycles and pivot points. Unlike trend indicators, DPO focuses on short-term movements, making it easier to find local highs and lows.\__

### How the Detrended Price Oscillator works:

DPO measures the difference between the price and the moving average (SMA). This indicator helps to identify overbought and oversold conditions and to detect recurring cycles.

Positive values 🟢 price is above the average (potential overbought zone).

Negative values 🔴 the price is below the average (potential oversold zone).

**Searching for reversal points:**

When DPO reaches a peak and starts to decline a downward reversal is possible.

**Identifying cycles:**

Use DPO to identify regular price cycles and predict future fluctuations.

**Signal Confirmation:**

Compare the DPO with the price movement. If the price updates the high and the DPO shows a lower value, a downward reversal is likely.

### Example of use on Pacifica

Imagine that an asset on the**Pacifica** has reached +1.5 on the DPO several times, followed by a downward reversal. As soon as the DPO approaches +1.5 again, it could be a signal to take profits or open a short.

If the DPO drops to -1.5 and reverses upwards, it could be a good buying opportunity.

**🚀 Tips for working effectively with DP**O

**Adjust the period**: Try different period values to adapt the indicator to your trading style.

**Watch for extreme values**: DPO extreme levels often indicate reversal potential.

**Use with other indicators**: Combine with RSI or MACD to confirm signals.

**Risk Management**: Set a stop loss near the nearest support and resistance levels.
