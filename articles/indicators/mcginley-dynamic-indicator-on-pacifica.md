What is McGinley Dynamic?

McGinley Dynamic is an indicator that is similar to a moving average, but much smarter and more accurate. It automatically adapts to changing price speeds, eliminating the lag that regular SMAs and EMAs have.

> This indicator was developed by trader John R. McGinley in the 1990s. Its goal is to make the line follow the price as closely as possible, avoiding sharp jerks.

_\_The main advantage of McGinley Dynamic is that it automatically speeds up when the market moves quickly and slows down when the price fluctuates sideways.\__

### How is McGinley Dynamic calculated?

**Where:**

MD_t — current value of McGinley Dynamic

MD_{t-1} — previous value

Price — current price

N — indicator period (usually 10, 20 or 50)

💡**The main difference from SMA/EMA**:

If the price rises or falls sharply, McGinley Dynamic accelerates and catches up with it. And if the market slows down, it also becomes slower.

How to interpret McGinley Dynamic?

1️⃣ Trend identification

If the price is above McGinley Dynamic → uptrend.

If the price is below McGinley Dynamic → downtrend.

2️⃣ Support and resistance identification

The McGinley Dynamic line acts as dynamic support in an uptrend.

If the price breaks it downwards → a reversal is possible.

3️⃣ Filtering out false signals

McGinley Dynamic eliminates the “noise” in the market and helps to ignore random price fluctuations.

Trading examples

Selling volumes are growing.

**❌ Solution:** Open a short, stop loss is above the McGinley line
