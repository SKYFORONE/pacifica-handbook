_\_Elder’s Force Index (EFI) is an indicator developed by Alexander Elder. It measures the strength of price movement using a combination of volume and price change. EFI helps to determine trend momentum, identify reversals and find entry and exit points.\__

### How the Elder’s Force Index works:

**Calculation Formula:**

EFI = (Current Closing Price Previous Closing Price) × Volume

A positive EFI indicates bullish momentum (buyers are dominant).

A negative EFI value indicates bearish pressure (sellers are stronger).

The higher the value, the greater the strength of the price movement.

### How to use Elder’s Force Index on Pacifica

**Trend Determination**:

If the EFI is positive and rising, it confirms an uptrend.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKc7mH5WgAAb9lc.jpg?name=large)

If the EFI is negative and declining, the market is in a downtrend.

### Finding entry points:

Buy when the EFI goes from a negative zone to a positive zone.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKc80xZW8AAwfJc.jpg?name=large)

Sell when the EFI drops below zero.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKc9PmyXIAAH2b2.jpg?name=large)

Divergence detection:

If the price makes a new high and the EFI drops a signal of a weakening trend.

### Example of use on Pacifica

Let’s say you are trading BTC on Pacifica and the EFI starts to rise sharply above 0. This confirms bullish strength and you can look for long entries. If the EFI approaches 0 or goes negative, it signals a loss of momentum, which could be the time to take profits.

🚀 Tips for working effectively with the EFI

**Combine with other indicators:**RSI, MACD or Bollinger Bands will help filter out false signals.

**Watch volume:**The higher the volume when the EFI is rising, the more reliable the signal.

**Use different periods:** A short period (2–10) gives quick signals, a long period (13–50) shows steady trends.
