_\_Chaikin Oscillator is an indicator created by Mark Chaikin. It helps to analyze the flow of money into the market and determine where the capital is moving to buy or sell. It is used to find reversals and trend confirmation.\__

### How the Chaikin Oscillator works

Based on the Accumulation/Distribution (A/D) Line, measures the rate of change in cash flow.

Represents the difference between the 3-period and 10-period EMA of the A/D Line.

It fluctuates around the zero line:

If above 0 the market is under bullish control (buyers are active).

If below 0 bears dominate (sellers are stronger).

Buy when Chaikin crosses above 0 and is confirmed by volume.

### Example of use on Pacifica

Let’s say you’re trading SOL and you see that Chaikin has crossed 0 upwards. This means that liquidity is entering the market and you can consider going long. If Chaikin falls below zero, this may be a signal to take profit or open a short position.

🚀 Tips for working effectively with Chaikin Oscillator

Don’t trade on one signal always check confirmations from other indicators.

Look at volumes Chaikin growth without volume support may be false.

Adapt the settings to the market different assets may require adjusting EMA periods.

> Chaikin Oscillator is a powerful tool for analyzing market liquidity. Use it on Pacifica to find early entry signals and assess the strength of price movement!
