What is Ichimoku Cloud?

Ichimoku Cloud is a universal indicator that shows:

✅ Trend (up or down)

✅ Support and resistance levels

✅ Trend strength

✅ Possible entry and exit points

This tool is especially popular in medium and long term trading due to its accuracy.

_**In this article, I will explain in simple terms how the indicator works on Pacifica, how to use it correctly and what you should pay attention to**_**.**

### How does Ichimoku Cloud work?

**The indicator has 5 lines, each of which plays its own role:**

1️⃣ Tenkan-sen (red line) short-term trend line

Calculated as the average between the maximum and minimum for the last 9 candles.

If the line rises, the trend is upward.

If it falls, the trend is downward.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKnTh3TWwAAGZ2W.jpg?name=large)

2️⃣ Kijun-sen (blue line) the main trend line

The average value between the maximum and minimum for 26 candles.

Works as a dynamic support and resistance level.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKnT9FBW4AEVH0M.jpg?name=large)

3️⃣ Senkou Span A (cloud top)

The average of Tenkan-sen and Kijun-sen, shifted forward by 26 candles.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKnUagBXYAEz9bb.jpg?name=large)

4️⃣ Senkou Span B (lower cloud boundary)

Average value between the maximum and minimum for 52 candles, shifted forward by 26 candles.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKnUlLfXIAAIoXj.jpg?name=large)

5️⃣Chikou Span (green line) — lagging line

Shows the price 26 candles ago, helps to see past trends.

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKnUvHqXoAAAtC5.jpg?name=large)

* * *

☁ Ichimoku Cloud (Kumo) is the space between Senkou Span A and B. It acts as support and resistance.

How to read Ichimoku Cloud?

📌**1. Trend definitio**n

If the price is above the cloud → uptrend.

If the price is below the cloud → downtrend.

If the price is inside the cloud → market is in uncertainty (flat).

**📌 2. Trend strength by the clou**d

Thick cloud → strong support/resistance.

Thin cloud → weak level, possible breakout.

�**� 3. Line intersections (entry signals**)

**✅ “Bullish” signal**:

Tenkan-sen crosses Kijun-sen upwards → buy signal.

Price leaves the cloud upwards → growth confirmation.

**❌ “Bearish” signal:**

Tenkan-sen crosses Kijun-sen downwards → sell signal.

Price falls below the cloud → decline confirmation.

### How to use Ichimoku Cloud in trading on Pacifica?

**📌 1. Trend trading strateg**y

If the price is above the cloud and Tenkan-sen is above Kijun-sen, the trend is strong. In such cases, it is better to look for purchases.

**✅ Example of a trade:**

The price breaks the top of the cloud.

Tenkan-sen crosses Kijun-sen upwards.

Chikou Span is above the price 26 candles ago → confirmation.

📌**Solution: Buy, stop-loss is below the cloud.** 📌

![Image 8](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKnVlHYXcAAibXL.jpg?name=large)

**📌 2. Cloud Breakout Strateg**y

If the price breaks the cloud downwards → a new downward trend is possible.

If the price goes up → the trend may change to bullish.

![Image 9](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKnV2oWWkAAjtFE.jpg?name=large)

* * *

📌 3. Determining stop loss and take profit levels

Stop loss can be placed outside the cloud boundaries.

Take profit at the nearest resistance or by the value of the previous movement.

Real trading examples

**🔹 Scenario 1**: Price breaks through the cloud upwards

Price rises above the cloud.

Tenkan-sen crosses Kijun-sen upwards.

Chikou Span is above the price 26 candles ago.

**✅ Solution:** Enter long, stop loss is below the cloud.

**🔹 Scenario 2**: Price breaks through the cloud downwards

Price goes below the cloud.

Tenkan-sen crosses Kijun-sen downwards.

Chikou Span is below the price 26 candles ago.

**❌ Solution:** Enter short, stop loss is above the cloud…

📌 Ichimoku Cloud is a powerful tool that shows the trend, support and entry/exit points.

✔ If the price is above the cloud, the trend is up.

✔ If the price is below the cloud, the trend is down.

✔ Crossovers of the lines give entry signals.
