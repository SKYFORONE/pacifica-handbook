_\_Relative Vigor Index (RVI) is an indicator that measures the strength and direction of a trend based on the ratio of the closing price to the range of fluctuations over a certain period. Its basic idea is that in a bull market the price usually closes closer to the high and in a bear market it closes closer to the low.\__

**Key operating principles:**

RVI above zero, signaling an uptrend.

RVI below zero, indicates a downtrend.

Crossing of the signal line helps to identify entry and exit points.

How the Relative Vigor Index works

RVI consists of two lines:

The main RVI line, reflects the current strength of the trend.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNfFvJXYAAWIax.jpg?name=large)

Signal line a smoothed version of the main line, used to find crossovers and confirm signals.

### How to use Relative Vigor Index on Pacifica

Finding entry and exit points:

Long (buy): When the RVI crosses the signal line from bottom to top, it is a buy signal.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNgOO3XEAEcLeX.jpg?name=large)

Short (sell): If RVI crosses the signal line from top to bottom, it is a signal to open a short position.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNhGgtWgAElgS_.jpg?name=large)

* * *

### Determination of trend strength

If the RVI is confidently above zero, this confirms an uptrend and the possibility of entering a long.

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNiJdWXQAAlB2f.jpg?name=large)

If the RVI is below zero, this is a bearish signal, you can look for a short.

**Divergences:**

If price updates a high and the RVI falls, this is a signal of a possible downward reversal.

![Image 8](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNja8cXQAA7lOH.jpg?name=large)

If the price is falling and the RVI is rising, expect an upward reversal.

**Exit the trade:**

**Close the position when the RVI reverses and crosses the signal line in the opposite direction.**

* * *

### on Pacifica

Imagine that on the Pacifica the RVI crossed the signal line from the bottom to the top and both indicators are above zero, this is a strong bullish signal to open a long position.

![Image 9](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNkJQbXQAA0yPf.jpg?name=large)

* * *

If after some time the RVI reverses and crosses the signal line from top to bottom, this is a reason to lock in profits and exit the trade.

🚀 Tips for trading with Relative Vigor Index

Adjust the parameters to the market: The standard value is a period of 10, but for short-term trading on**Pacifica**you can use 5–7 for faster signals.

* * *

Combine with other indicators: Use MACD or Moving Average to confirm the trend and filter out false signals.

Look for Divergences: Divergences between price and RVI often predict reversals.

Risk Management: Set a stop loss behind the nearest extreme to limit losses.
