### What is Pivot Points Standard?

Pivot Points are levels that are calculated based on yesterday’s data:

Maximum

Minimum

Close

The main idea is that the price often bounces off these levels or breaks through them, forming a strong move.

**The indicator has several lines:**

The main Pivot Point (PP) is the central point from which other levels are calculated.

**R1, R2, R3**(**Resistance**) are resistance levels.

**S1, S2, S3 (Support**) are support levels.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKmu3e5WYAAwBRL.png?name=large)

### How are Pivot Points calculated?

Central Pivot Point (PP)

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKmvdiKWYAA8aVE.jpg?name=large)

**High** — yesterday’s maximum

**Low** — yesterday’s minimum

**Close** — yesterday’s closing price

2️⃣ Resistance levels

* **R1 = (PP × 2) — Low**
* **R2 = PP + (High — Low)**
* **R3 = High + 2 × (PP — Low)**

3️⃣ Support levels

* **S1 = (PP × 2) — High**
* **S2 = PP — (High — Low)**
* **S3 = Low — 2 × (High — PP)**

### How to interpret Pivot Points?

1. Determining the trend

If the price is above PP, then the trend is upward.

### How to use Pivot Points in trading?

**Rebound Trading Strategy**

If the price touches a level (S1, S2, R1, R2) and does not break through it, you can consider entering:

**Buy from S1 or S2 (if the trend is up).**

![Image 11](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKmzIikWQAA4DN4.jpg?name=large)

Sell ​​from R1 or R2 (if the trend is downward).

![Image 12](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKmz1iVXAAIgPN-.jpg?name=large)

**✅ Example of a trade:**

The price falls to S1, but does not break the level.

Additionally, we look at volumes or RSI.

If the signal is confirmed, we open a long with a short stop loss.

📌 2. Breakout trading strategy

If the price confidently breaks through the R1 or S1 level, this may be a signal of trend continuation.

**✅ Example of a trade:**

The price broke through R1 and consolidated above.

We look at the volumes — if they grow, the movement is strong.

We open a long with a target at R2.

📌 3. Defining stop losses and take profits

> 📌 Stop loss can be placed behind the nearest Pivot Points level.

![Image 13](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKm0DntWIAAWFGr.jpg?name=large)
