How CMO works:

The indicator formula looks like this:

**CMO**= [(Sum of positive changes Sum of negative changes) / (Sum of positive changes + Sum of negative changes)] × 100

**CMO** values range from -100 to +100.

If the **CMO** is above +50, it indicates a strong uptrend.

If the **CMO** is below -50, it indicates a strong downtrend.

Crossing the level 0 can be a signal of a trend change.

* * *

### How to use CMO on Pacifica

**Trend Determination:**

If the CMO is above +50, the market is in a strong bullish trend.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKcqWxlWcAAHhrq.jpg?name=large)

If the CMO is below -50, a bearish trend prevails.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKcq2dUXYAE90ZY.jpg?name=large)

### Detecting overbought and oversold conditions:

If the CMO is approaching +100, the asset may be overbought and a pullback is possible.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKcr2kiWAAAhlHh.jpg?name=large)

If the CMO approaches +100, the asset may be oversold

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKcsfeMXkAANWUF.jpg?name=large)

### Searching for divergences:

If price makes a new high and the CMO stays below the previous values, a downward reversal is possible.

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKcsklgXAAALLeA.jpg?name=large)

### Strategies for trading with CMOs on Pacifica

**Trend Trading:**

Buy if the CMO is above 0 and moving up.

Sell if the CMO is below 0 and moving down.

### Example of use on Pacifica

Imagine that on Pacifica the asset started to rise and the CMO broke +50. This confirms that the trend is strong and you can look for long entry points.

If the price is approaching its peak and the CMO starts to decline, this is a signal of a possible reversal, and you should think about profit taking or entering short.

**🚀 Tips for working effectively with CMO**s

**Don’t use CMO alone**: Combine it with trend indicators like EMA or Bollinger Bands.

**Watch out for ±50 levels**: They help to identify overbought and oversold moments.

**Check for divergences**: They often provide early signals of a trend reversal.
