_\_Rate of Change (ROC) is an indicator that measures the percentage change in price over a certain period. It helps to identify the strength and direction of the trend, as well as to find potential reversal points.\__

![Image 1](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNoJgXWAAAEJeU.jpg?name=large)

### How to add an indicator to Pacifica

1. Open the**Pacifica**
2. Click on the indicators tab
3. search for the name of the indicator Rate of Change

![Image 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNo36NXsAAjKBD.jpg?name=large)

* * *

**Key operating principles:**

Positive ROC value indicates an uptrend.

A negative ROC value signals a downtrend.

Divergences between the price and ROC can herald a market reversal.

How Rate of Change works

Positive value, the market is growing.

Negative value, the market is falling.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNpvayXEAAm_Pc.jpg?name=large)

The higher the ROC value, the stronger the price movement. Sharp changes may indicate overbought or oversold.

* * *

### how to use Rate of Change on Pacifica

Trend Definition:

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNqci5WgAA_ZO2.jpg?name=large)

ROC below zero, indicates a downtrend. The lower the value, the stronger the selling pressure.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNrHs9XkAAWtnl.jpg?name=large)

* * *

### Finding entry and exit points:

Long (buy): When the ROC crosses the zero line from bottom to top, it is a signal to enter a long position.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNrnKWWgAEu5Q1.jpg?name=large)

Short (Sell): When the ROC crosses the zero line from top to bottom, it is a signal to enter a short position.

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNsecOXMAAWAct.jpg?name=large)

* * *

**Divergences:**

Bullish Divergence: When the price makes a new low and the ROC goes up this is a signal for an upward reversal.

Bearish Divergence: When the price makes a new high and the ROC goes down, it is a signal for a downward reversal.

![Image 8](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNtV0ZWUAAN1gc.jpg?name=large)

* * *

### Practical example on Pacifica

Let’s assume that on the Pacifica the price of an asset has started to grow and ROC has crossed the zero line from below upwards. This confirms the upward momentum, you can open a long position.

If after some time ROC starts to decline and crosses the zero line back, this is a signal to take profit and exit the position.

* * *

### Tips for trading with Rate of Change

**Adjust the parameters to the market:**

For a quick trade, use **9–10** periods.

For long term trading, use**20–25** periods.

Combine with other indicators: Use Moving Average or RSI to confirm signals and avoid false entries.

Watch out for divergences: They often predict a trend reversal.

Risk Management: Place a stop loss at the nearest support/resistance levels to protect your capital.
