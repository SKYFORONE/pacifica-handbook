**What is Money Flow Index (MFI)?**

The Money Flow Index (MFI) is a technical indicator that shows the strength of the flow of money into an asset, taking into account not only price but also volume. It is often called “RSI with volume” — because it is similar to the Relative Strength Index (RSI), but it also uses trading volume in its calculations.

**How does the indicator work?**

MFI analyzes the movement of money: inflows and outflows of capital into a particular asset. It helps to determine:

Is the asset being bought or sold on increased volume?

Is there hidden pressure on the price?

Is there an overbought or oversold condition?

### 💡 How to use MFI in trading on Pacifica

**1️⃣ Finding pivot points**

If MFI enters overbought/oversold zones, a reversal can be expected:

Enter short when MFI > 80 and signs of reversal.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLadefpXAAAfqkE.jpg?name=large)

Long entry when MFI < 20 and signals for growth.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLadkeDXcAAX_7K.jpg?name=large)

**2️⃣ Trend confirmation**

MFI can confirm trend strength:

MFI growth together with the price is a signal that growth is supported by volume.

A falling MFI with a rising price is a signal that the trend is weak.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLaeoSSWEAARYFB.jpg?name=large)

**3️⃣ Divergences**

Bullish divergence: price makes a new low, but MFI does not. An upward reversal is possible.

Bearish divergence: price makes a new high and MFI declines. A downward reversal is possible

✅ Advantages and ❌ Disadvantages

**✅ Advantages:**

Accounts for volume, unlike RSI.

Helps to identify hidden forces in the market.

Works well in range trading.

**❌ Disadvantages:**

May lag in the trend.

Does not always give clear signals without other confirmations.

Requires complex analysis: best used in conjunction with levels, candlestick patterns or other indicators.

Money Flow Index is a powerful tool for assessing the strength of price movement with volume. It is especially useful for finding reversals and divergences. However, like any indicator, it requires competent application and does not replace a full-fledged market analysis.
