_\_Advance/Decline Indicator (A/D) is a market indicator that shows the balance between rising and falling assets. It helps to determine the general direction of the market and identify divergences that signal a possible reversal.\__

### How the Advance/Decline Indicator works:

The indicator is calculated as the difference between the number of stocks (or assets) that have risen in value (advances) and the number that have fallen (declines):

A/D = Number of rising assets Number of falling assets

If the A/D value is rising, it signals a bullish market sentiment most assets are rising.

If the A/D value is falling, it indicates a bearish sentiment most assets are declining.

### How to use A/D Indicator on Pacifica

**Determining the overall trend:**

A rising A/D confirms an uptrend.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKX9EfHXkAAIXYG.jpg?name=large)

A falling A/D confirms the downtrend.

* * *

### Identifying divergences:

If the price of an asset is rising but the A/D is falling, this may be a downward reversal signal.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKX-YluXQAA2zNF.jpg?name=large)

If the price falls and the A/D rises, an upward reversal is expected.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKX--k8XEAASOWR.jpg?name=large)

### Trading Strategies with A/D on Pacifica

**Trend following:**

Buy if the A/D rises with the price this is confirmation of an uptrend.

![Image 8](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKYANrzWMAAWrEl.jpg?name=large)

Sell if the A/D falls and the price goes down confirmation of a downtrend.

![Image 9](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKYBbBnWQAApSvX.jpg?name=large)

### Example of use on Pacifica

Suppose you see that on the Pacifica platform the price of an asset is rising, but the A/D is not updating new tops. This is a signal that the rise may be false and you should be careful with longs.

If the price is falling and the A/D is rising, it could be a great opportunity to enter a long while waiting for a reversal.

🚀 Tips for working effectively with the A/D Indicator

**Watch for divergences**: Divergences often predict major reversals.

**Use in conjunction with trend indicators:** For example, EMA to confirm direction.

**Check volume:** A/D gives more accurate signals as volume increases.
