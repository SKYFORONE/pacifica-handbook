What is Median Price?

Median Price is an indicator that shows the average price of a candle. It helps smooth out market fluctuations and simplify trend analysis.

Unlike regular moving averages or complex indicators, Median Price uses a simple formula:

![Image 1](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKnoxTyWEAEJ01w.png?name=large)

**Where:**

High maximum of the candle.

Low minimum of the candle.

👉 That is, Median Price shows where the “center” of the price is for the period.

What is Median Price used for?

📌 1. Trend detection

If the median price is rising, the market is ascending.

If the median price is falling, the market is descending.

**📌 2. Smoothing out market nois**e

Median Price removes excess “noise”, which makes the chart more readable.

**📌 3. Basis for other indicator**s

Many strategies are built on the basis of Median Price, for example, median moving averages.

How to interpret the Median Price?

🔹 1. Rising median price → uptrend

If the median price is consistently higher than previous values, then the trend is bullish.

✅ What to do? Consider buying.

🔹 2. Falling median price → downtrend

If the median price is falling, this is a signal for a possible downward movement.

❌ What to do? Consider shorts or profit taking.

🔹 3. Using Median Price with moving averages

If the price is above the median moving average (e.g. EMA 50) → the trend is bullish.

If the price is below → the trend is bearish.

### How to use Median Price in trading on Pacifica?

Filter for entering a trade

If the price is higher than the Median Price → we are looking for purchases only.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKnq3mYWEAAxbTS.jpg?name=large)

If below → we are looking for sales only.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKnrPb9WkAApq-d.jpg?name=large)

**Determining entry and exit points**

If the price crosses the median moving average, this may be an entry or exit signal.

**✅ Example of a trade:**

The price has broken through the median line upwards.

RSI shows growth.

Open a long, stop loss below the minimum.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKnr38ZXoAAkPuX.jpg?name=large)

**📌 3. Identifying support and resistance level**s

If the median price does not break through for a long time, this is a strong level.

When the price breaks through the level, a trend breakout is possible.

Trading examples

🔹 Scenario 1: Uptrend

Price is above Median Price.

50-day EMA is also rising.

Volumes are increasing.

✅ Solution: Enter long, set stop loss below median price.
