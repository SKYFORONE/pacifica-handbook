_\_Keltner Channel is a volatility indicator that shows price dynamics and helps to determine entry and exit points. It consists of three lines\__

![Image 1](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNVSLNXYAAdjDe.jpg?name=large)

### How to add an indicator to Pacifica

1. Open the[Pacifica](https://app.pacifica.fi?referral=SKYFOR)
2. Click on the indicators tab
3. search for the name of the indicator Keltner Channel

![Image 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNWN49W0AAhSKK.jpg?name=large)

Average line (EMA), shows the main trend direction.

Upper and lower boundaries, calculated on the basis of ATR (Average True Range) and shows the range of volatility.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNW3x1WwAATCnv.jpg?name=large)

**Key operating principles:**

Price above the channel, strong uptrend.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNXWTwXYAAjE1U.jpg?name=large)

Price below the channel strong downtrend.

Price inside the channel flat or weak trend.

### How to use Keltner Channel on Pacifica

**Determining the trend direction:**

If the price is moving along the upper boundary, this is a bullish trend signal.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNX3L8XQAA0RSE.jpg?name=large)

If the price touches or breaks the lower boundary, it indicates a bearish trend.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNYRIFWkAAM1lf.jpg?name=large)

Finding entry and exit points:

Long (buy): When the price breaks the upper boundary, a signal to enter a long position.

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNZWnhXgAAlflg.jpg?name=large)

Short (sell): If price crosses the lower boundary, open a short position.

![Image 8](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKNaAOCXoAAzp4D.jpg?name=large)

**Trading on pullbacks:**

If price returns to the EMA midline after going outside the boundary, this could be a trend entry point.

### Practical example on Pacifica

Let’s assume that on the Pacifica platform the price broke through the upper boundary of the Keltner Channel. This is a buy signal.

If the price starts to fall and crosses the middle line, it can be a signal to take profits.

**Keltner Channel**

Adjust parameters to the market: Standard settings are EMA (20) and ATR (2). For active markets, increase ATR to 2.5–3.

**Combine with other indicators**: Use RSI or MACD to confirm breakouts and reversals.

**Filter out false signals**: Ignore breakdowns, if volume is low, wait for confirmation by a high volume move.

**Risk Management**: Place a stop loss outside the channel boundary to minimize losses on a pullback.

> Keltner Channel is a universal indicator for trading on Pacifica which helps to catch trends and fix profits in time. Use it in combination with other tools to increase the accuracy of signals!
