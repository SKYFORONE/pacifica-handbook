**What is Volatility Zero Trend Close-to-Close?**

Volatility Zero Trend Close-to-Close is a technical indicator designed to measure market volatility by eliminating the influence of the overall trend. Unlike traditional methods, which can be distorted by directional price movement, this indicator focuses solely on the volatility of prices relative to their average.

**🔧 How does the indicator work**?

The indicator analyzes consecutive closing prices over a given period, neutralizing the effect of a persistent uptrend or downtrend. This allows traders to get a more accurate picture of the pure volatility of an asset without being distracted by the overall market direction.

### Indicator Settings and Parameters

When setting up the Volatility Zero Trend Close-to-Close indicator, it is important to consider the following parameters:

Calculation Period: Determines the number of consecutive closing prices used to analyze volatility.

Line Color (plot.color): The color of the indicator line to display.

Line Style (plot.linestyle): The type of line (solid, dotted, etc.).

Line Width (plot.linewidth): The thickness of the displayed line.

Display Type (plot.plottype): The form of data visualization (line, histogram, etc.).

Transparency (plot.transparency): The transparency level of the indicator line.

These parameters can be customized depending on the trader’s preferences and the trading platform used.

### Interpretation of the indicator signals

High indicator values: Indicate increased volatility, which may indicate potential trading opportunities or the need to adjust the strategy.

Low indicator values: Indicate low volatility, which may signal market stability or the absence of significant movements.

Sharp upward jumps: Indicate that the market is entering a period of high activity, which may lead to strong trend movements.

Long stay at low levels: May signal consolidation before an impulsive movement.

It is important to remember that the Volatility Zero Trend Close-to-Close indicator does not predict the price direction, but only estimates the degree of its volatility.

✅ Advantages and ❌ Disadvantages

Suitable for various markets (stocks, forex, cryptocurrencies).

❌ Disadvantages:

Can give late signals during periods of high activity.
