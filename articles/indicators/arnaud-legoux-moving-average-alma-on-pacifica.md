_\_Arnaud Legoux Moving Average (ALMA) is an improved version of the moving average, designed to smoothen price more accurately and minimize lag. Unlike standard moving averages (SMA or EMA), ALMA uses a special algorithm that makes the indicator more sensitive to price changes while keeping the line smooth.\__

### How ALMA works:

The indicator is based on a weighted moving average, where the weights are distributed according to a Gaussian function. This allows ALMA to react quickly to price movements without creating excessive noise.

If the price is above the ALMA line, it indicates an uptrend (a buy signal).

If the price is below the ALMA line, it indicates a downtrend (sell signal).

### How to use ALMA on Pacifica

**Determining the trend direction:**

When the price is above the ALMA and the line is sloping upwards the trend is bullish.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKXpy_dXcAAllZE.jpg?name=large)

When the price is below the ALMA and the line is sloping downward the trend is bearish.

### Example of use on Pacifica

Imagine that an asset on the**Pacifica**has crossed ALMA from the bottom to the top several times. This confirms an uptrend and you can open a long position. If the price crosses the ALMA from top to bottom, this is a signal to take profits or open a short.

🚀 Tips for working effectively with the ALMA

**Customize the parameters**: Experiment with the window length (usually 9, 21 or 50) and offset to adapt the indicator to your trading style.

**Use with other tools**: ALMA works well with RSI to identify entry and exit points.

**Don’t ignore the trend**: In strong trends ALMA gives the most accurate signals, avoid trading against the main movement.
