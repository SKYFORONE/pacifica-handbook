_\_SuperTrend Indicator is a simple but powerful tool that helps to determine the trend direction and gives clear signals for entering and exiting the market. It is based on ATR (Average True Range), which allows you to take volatility into account and adapt to market conditions.\__

### How SuperTrend Indicator works

**The indicator is displayed as a line that moves along the price**:

Green line uptrend (buy signal).

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIL12tXkAAvEdV.jpg?name=large)

Red line downtrend (sell signal)

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIMQGnXcAADn5j.jpg?name=large)

**Key operating principles:**

If the price crosses the line from bottom to top, the indicator changes color to green a buy signal.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIMZxjW0AAVNhw.jpg?name=large)

If the price crosses the line from top to bottom, the line becomes red a sell signal.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIMp5iWEAAUQnf.jpg?name=large)

* * *

### How to use SuperTrend Indicator on Pacifica

**Long (buy) Open a position when the line changes color to green and sits below the price.**

![Image 7](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKINCeZXwAAEvmn.jpg?name=large)

Short (Sell): Enter a short position when the line changes color to red and is above the price.

![Image 8](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKINbY_XEAA9oP1.jpg?name=large)

* * *

**Filter out false signals:**

Use a higher timeframe (H1 and above) for reliable signals.

![Image 9](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIOLL2XQAAAlgn.png?name=large)

* * *

### Confirm signals with MACD or RSI to avoid false entries.

![Image 10](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIO0zuWYAAnC3J.jpg?name=large)

**Exit the trade:**

Close the position when the price crosses the line and the indicator changes color.

![Image 11](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIPEO1WEAAp6cz.jpg?name=large)

* * *

### Practical example on Pacifica

Let’s assume that on the Pacifica the price crosses the SuperTrend from bottom to top and the line turns green this is a buy signal.

![Image 12](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIPUOwWAAAL_ah.jpg?name=large)

If the price reverses and crosses the line from top to bottom, it turns red this is a signal for profit taking and possible short entry.

![Image 13](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HKIPnYcWkAA00hb.jpg?name=large)

Tips for trading with SuperTrend Indicator

Adjust the parameters to the market: The standard settings are a period of 14 and a multiplier of 3, but for more volatile assets on Pacifica you can increase the multiplier to 4.

Combine with other indicators: For example, use EMA for trend confirmation or Stochastic for overbought/oversold.

Risk Management: Always place a stop loss behind the SuperTrend line to minimize losses in case of a reversal.
