What is Parabolic SAR?

Parabolic SAR (Stop And Reverse) is a trend indicator that helps identify reversal points. It can be used both for entering and exiting a trade.

How does it work?

📌 When dots are below the price → the trend is up, we look for purchases.

📌 When dots are above the price → the trend is down, we look for sales.

👉 Main idea: Parabolic SAR follows the price and shows when the trend may reverse.

### How does Parabolic SAR work?

🔹 The indicator is calculated using a complex formula, but you need to understand the main thing:

The closer the dots are to the price, the stronger the trend.

When the dots change position (from bottom to top or vice versa), this is a signal for a reversal.

**📌 Parabolic SAR settings**:

Step (step) the standard value is 0.02 (the higher, the more sensitive the indicator).

Maximum (maximum) the standard value is 0.2 (determines how quickly the dots catch up with the price).

👉 If you increase Step, the indicator will react faster, but may give more false signals.

### How to interpret Parabolic SAR signals?

**🔹 1. Following the tren**d

If the dots are below the price **the trend is up**.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLQLDGXXsAAyuDh.jpg?name=large)

If the dots are above the price t**he trend is down.**

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLQLpRzX0AAayxG.jpg?name=large)

📌 Parabolic SAR works great in a trending market, but gives false signals in a flat!

**🔹 2. Trend reversa**l

When the dots jump to the other side, this may mean a change in trend.

The earlier the SAR changes, the weaker the trend.

**🔹 3. Using as a trailing sto**p

You can set a stop loss based on the SAR dots.

If the trend continues, the stop loss automatically moves with the price.

### How to use Parabolic SAR in trading on Pacifica?

**1. Trading with the trend**

Parabolic SAR helps to hold the position until the trend changes.

**Example of a trade:**

The price is above Parabolic SAR.

MACD confirms the uptrend.

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLQM7E1WgAA-k6a.jpg?name=large)

**📌 We close the trade when SAR changes sides**!

* * *

**📌 2. Finding reversal point**s

If the price has been moving in one direction for a long time, a change in the SAR position may indicate a trend reversal.

**✅ Example of a trade:**

SAR was below the price, but moved up.

RSI shows overbought.

Open a short, stop loss above the last maximum.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLQOWPpXcAAxCSK.jpg?name=large)

**Using with other indicators**

Parabolic SAR is best combined with RSI, MACD and moving averages.

Example:

If SAR gives a buy signal + RSI is above 50 → the signal is stronger.

If SAR shows a reversal, but MACD is still growing → we wait for confirmation.

* * *

**Pros and cons of Parabolic SAR**

Pros:

✔ Easy to read signals.

✔ Good for trend strategies.

✔ Can be used as a trailing stop.

**Cons:**

✖ Gives false signals in flat.

✖ Does not show the strength of the trend.

**💡 Tip**: Use Parabolic SAR on H1 and above to avoid noise!

**Parabolic SAR is a powerful tool for following the trend and finding exit points.**

✔ If SAR is below the price → uptrend, look for purchases.

✔ If SAR is above the price → downtrend, look for sales.

✔ SAR gives the best results in combination with other indicators!

**💡 Tip**: Use SAR together with RSI, MACD or moving averages to confirm signals.
