**What are these figures?**

Ascending and descending triangles are classic trend continuation patterns. They occur when the market has taken a breather and is gaining strength to continue moving in the same direction.

🔺 Ascending triangle, signals growth.

🔻 Descending triangle,more often indicates a fall.

_**In this article, I will explain in simple terms how the pattern works on Pacifica, how to use it correctly and what you should pay attention to**_**.**

![Image 1](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMIrSZWXUAAtjUL.jpg?name=large)

* * *

**What does an ascending triangle look like?**

From above a horizontal resistance level.

From below an ascending support line (lows are rising).

Price is **bumping**upwards more and more, but can’t break through yet.

As a result ' an upward explosion.

![Image 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMIr1j-WgAAatmk.jpg?name=large)

**✅ Example:**

The 100 level is resistance that won’t let up.

The lows are rising: 95 → 96.5 → 98 → 99.2

The market **squeezes** and shoots above 100.

**What does a descending triangle look like?**

From the bottom a horizontal support level.

Above a descending resistance line (highs are falling).

Sellers are pressing and buyers are defending the same level.

The bottom line is a downward breakout.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMItlNuXYAApCrk.jpg?name=large)

**✅ Example:**

Level 80 keeps the price from falling.

Highs are declining: 85 → 83 → 82 → 81

When buyers don’t hold out price breaks through 80 to the downside.

**💡 How to trade triangles**?

1️⃣ Waiting for a breakdown

No guessing. We act only after the exit from the triangle.

2️⃣ Enter the trade

Enter in the direction of the breakdown.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMIuMnoWgAAcw0N.jpg?name=large)

You can wait for a retest (return to the line) more conservative entry.

3️⃣ Stop Loss

Behind the opposite side of the triangle.

Or just behind the last maximum/minimum.

4️⃣ Take Profit

The height of the triangle at its base → postpone to the side of the breakdown.

**📊 Example: ascending triangl**e

Resistance: 150

Support: rising from 140 to 148

Breakout at 151 → long entry

Stop: under 148

Target: 150–140 = 10 → 151 + 10 = 161

**📉 Example: descending triangl**e

Support: 90

Resistance: down from 100 to 92

Breakout at 89 → enter short

Stop: above 92

Target: 100–90 = 10 → 89–10 = 79

**✅ Advantages and ❌ Disadvantages**

✅ Pros:

Clear structure.

Easy entry and clear risk.

Work in any market and timeframe.

**❌ Minuses:**

Sometimes the breakout is false specially without volumes.

It is not always easy to recognize the pattern in advance.

Triangles are the tension before the shot. It’s like the market is compressing a spring, and the longer the energy builds up, the stronger the movement on the way out.

> always look at volumes. If they are growing on a breakdown, it’s a powerful signal.
