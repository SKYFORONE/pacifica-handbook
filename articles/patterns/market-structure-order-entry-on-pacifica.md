**What is a Double Top?**

“Double top” is a classic graphical trend reversal pattern that appears at the top of an uptrend. It indicates that the bulls are tired and the sellers are starting to retake the initiative.

Visually, it is two peaks standing at approximately the same level, with a small decline between them. After the second peak, the price usually reverses downwards.

**How does the pattern form?**

The price grows and reaches the first peak, the maximum of the trend.

Then there is a pullback down (but not a collapse, just a correction).

After that, the market tries to break the high again, forming the second top, but fails to consolidate above it.

Price starts to fall and breaks the base line, the so-called neckline.

Breakdown of the neckline downwards = signal to open a short.

💡 How to trade a double top on Pacifica?

1️⃣ Entering short

We enter after breakdown of the neckline downwards.

![Image 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLpbNjaWIAAJEbc.jpg?name=large)

You can enter immediately or wait for the level retest from below (more reliable).

**Stop Loss**

We put it above the second top (or slightly above the first one, if they are almost at the same level).

**Take Profit**

Measure the distance from the top to the neck line.

Postpone the same distance down from the breakdown point — this is the target level.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLpbxBKXsAAIWHQ.jpg?name=large)

✅ Example:

First vertex: 120

Trough (neck line): 110

Second peak: 119

Breakdown of the neckline downwards → entry

Target: 110 — (120–110) = 100

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLpcf6xWYAA918E.jpg?name=large)

**🧠 How to improve accuracy**?

Volumes: volume is high at the first top and lower at the second top. This indicates bullish weakness.

RSI or MACD: if there is a divergence on the second top, it strengthens the signal.

Shape: the more symmetrical the tops are, the more reliable the pattern is.

**✅ Advantages and ❌ Disadvantages**

**✅ Advantages:**

Clear and visually clear signal.

Works on any timeframe.

Gives an excellent risk/profit ratio.

**❌ Disadvantages:**

Can be a false breakout if the market “cheats”.

Sometimes hard to distinguish from a flat or triangle.

Requires patience, pattern is not formed in 5 minutes.

A double top is one of the most reliable patterns that lets you know that the market has had enough of growth. If you see two similar tops and price breaks a key level from below, it’s your chance to short with a reasonable stop and a clear target.

**📌 Tip:** don’t hurry to enter “by eye” wait for the breakdown of the neckline, it is the basis of the pattern.
