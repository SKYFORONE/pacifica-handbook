> When a market tests a critical price level three times and fails to break through, it signals an exhaustion of buying or selling pressure. Triple Tops and Triple Bottoms represent decisive institutional distribution or accumulation.

### Understanding the Mechanics

* **Triple Top:** A bearish reversal pattern featuring three distinct peaks reaching approximately the same price ceiling, separated by two interim troughs. It proves bulls cannot absorb the overhead liquidity.
* **Triple Bottom:** A bullish reversal pattern featuring three distinct troughs reaching the same floor, separated by two interim reaction highs.

The critical structural pivot of both patterns is the **Neckline** (the horizontal support or resistance created by the interim peaks/valleys).

---

### How to Trade Triple Tops on Pacifica Perps

1. **Spot the 3 Peaks:** Identify three rejections at resistance on the 15m or 1h chart at [app.pacifica.fi](https://app.pacifica.fi?referral=SKYFOR).
2. **Draw the Neckline:** Connect the low points of the two pullbacks between the peaks.
3. **Wait for Breakdown Confirmation:** Do NOT anticipate the top. Wait for a full candle body close beneath the Neckline.
4. **Enter Short:** Open your short perp position with an invalidation stop loss placed above Peak 3.
5. **Target:** Measure the height from the highest peak to the neckline; project that distance downward from the breakdown point.

---

### How to Trade Triple Bottoms on Pacifica Perps

1. Confirm three rejections at the support floor.
2. Draw the horizontal Neckline across the reaction highs.
3. Enter Long upon a verified close above the Neckline accompanied by expanding volume.
4. Stop Loss placed safely beneath the triple bottom troughs.

---

### Common Traps
* **Premature entries:** Entering before neckline confirmation frequently results in getting chopped out during range extension.
* **Liquidation hunting:** On high-leverage perps, wick hunts beyond the peaks occur often. Always place your stop with a small buffer above the wicks!
