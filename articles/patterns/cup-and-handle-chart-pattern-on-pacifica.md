**What is this pattern?**

**Three White Soldiers** is a candlestick pattern that appears at the bottom of a downtrend or after a correction and signals the beginning of a strong growth.

�**� Imagine**

the market has been falling for a long time, then one green candle appears… then a second, then a third, each closing higher than the previous one. That’s it the “three soldiers” marching steadily upward.

_**In this article, I will explain in simple terms how the pattern works on Pacifica, how to use it correctly and what you should pay attention to**_**.**

![Image 1](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMyI97yXcAAr30-.jpg?name=large)

* * *

**🔍 What does the pattern look like**?

Three bullish (green) candles in a row.

Each candle opens inside the body of the previous candle and closes above its close.

All three candles have relatively long bodies and small shadows.

Appears against the background of a reversal or the end of a correction.

> 📌 Visually looks like three confident steps up. And the cleaner the better.

![Image 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMyJyGtXEAAQfRf.jpg?name=large)

**🧠 What does that tell us**?

The market has stopped falling.

Buyers are steadily stepping in and driving the price up day after day.

This often indicates a change of mood from fear to buying.

* * *

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMyKM4YWUAALsg5.png?name=large)

**📊 Where does it work best**?

After a strong drop as an upward reversal.

On daily charts more reliable than on lower timeframes.

Combined with support levels, RSI in oversold zone, etc.

* * *

**💡 How to trade by pattern**?

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HMyKsLtWoAAuEWR.jpg?name=large)

1️⃣ Entry

You can enter long right after the third candle closes.

Or wait for a small pullback to the body of the second candle (conservative).

2️⃣ Stop Loss

Set it just below the minimum of the first candle (or under the whole formation).

3️⃣ Take profit

The first target is the nearest resistance.

Then you can follow the trend, pulling up the stop.,

* * *

**✅ Example:**

The price was falling from 150 to 125.

Then the first green candle appears closing at 128.

The second opening 128, closing 131.

The third opening 131, closing 134.

Entry after the third candle.

Stop under 125, targe140 and above.

❗️На what to pay attention to

If the shadows are long it is not “soldiers”, but uncertainty.

If the candles are quite short it may be noise.

Volumes: it is good if the volume grows on the third candle it confirms the interest of buyers.

**✅ Advantages and ❌ Disadvantages**

**✅ Advantages:**

Clear and visually clear signal.

Works well at the beginning of new trends.

Easy to use.

**❌ Disadvantages:**

May not work on news or flat.

Requires confirmation by other indicators or levels.

Not always suitable for short-term trades.

“Three White Soldiers” is not just candles. It is the psychology of the market, when sellers give up and buyers start steadily advancing. It is a strong signal, especially after a strong decline and near support.

> 📌 don’t trade this pattern “in the void” look at what’s around you: level, volume, context. And then the three soldiers will lead you to a good trade 💹
