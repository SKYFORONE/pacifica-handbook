📌 What is a Bearish Diamond Top?

The Bearish Diamond Top is a chart reversal pattern that forms at the top of an uptrend and signals that a possible decline is ahead.

📐 The pattern got this name because on the chart it really looks like a diamond: first the movement expands, then narrows, forming a “diamond”.

👉 The main thing is that a bearish rhombus most often portends the end of growth and a downward reversal.

🔍 How does the pattern form?

The pattern starts to expand, the price makes higher and lower peaks (as if “swinging”).

Then the movement starts to narrow, the peaks and troughs become closer to each other.

The result is a rhombus shape, wide at the beginning and narrow at the end.

The key moment is the breakdown of the lower boundary. This is a signal to short.

🧠 The trick is that inside the rhombus the market looks chaotic and uncertain, but when it leaves the figure, it can move strongly.

### How to recognize a “bearish rhombus” on a chart?

**Previous trend: uptrend.**

The pattern looks like a widening triangle transitioning into a narrowing triangle.

Price makes different-sized tops and troughs, first wider, then narrower.

A downward breakout is a signal to open a short position.

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLpQ_gGXYAAputt.jpg?name=large)

* * *

**💡 How to use in trading on Pacifica**?

**Entering short**

Wait for the price to exit the rhombus downwards and close below the lower boundary.

Enter a short position (sell/short).

It is desirable that the breakout is accompanied by an increase in volume — this will strengthen the signal.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLpRSN6WwAAtUD1.jpg?name=large)

**2️⃣ Stop loss**

We put it just above the last local peak inside the rhombus.

Or above the “neck” of the rhombus (the highest point of the figure).

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLpRtK1WUAAVfQy.jpg?name=large)

**3️⃣ Take Profit**

The minimum target is the height of the diamond set back down from the breakdown point.

Additionally, support/Fibonacci levels can be used.

![Image 6](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLpSFC_WUAAITjr.jpg?name=large)

**Case Study**

Let’s imagine we have an asset that has been growing for a long time, the price has reached the level of 1.5000:

**A chaotic movement starts:**

Peaks: 1.5100 → 1.5200 → 1.5050

Troughs: 1.4900 → 1.4800 → 1.4950

A **diamond** is formed on the chart (wide at the beginning, narrow at the end).

The price breaks through the level of 1.4900 downwards, enter short.

We put the stop at 1.5200, take profit at 1.4600 (according to the height of the diamond).

**As a result: competent entry, clear risk, clear target..**

✅ Advantages and Disadvantages

**Pros:**

Clear and visually clear pattern.

Works on all timeframes (from minutes to days).

Good potential for movement after breakout.

**Minuses:**

Often confused with other patterns (triangle, flag).

Can be a false breakout, especially if there are no volumes.

Requires patience, the figure does not form quickly.

The bearish rhombus is a great tool in a trader’s arsenal to find the end of a trend and the beginning of a decline. The main thing is not to hurry, wait for a clear structure and a confident downside breakout.

> use volumes and confirmation from other indicators (e.g. MACD or RSI) to make sure you are entering a real reversal and not a false signal.
