**What is “Head and Shoulders”?**

“Head and Shoulders” is a classic reversal pattern that signals that an uptrend may be ending and a downtrend may be beginning. It is one of the most reliable signals in technical analysis.

**Why such a name?** Because the chart does form a pattern that looks like two tops (shoulders) on the sides and one higher top (head) in the middle.

**What does the pattern consist of?**

**Left shoulder,** price rises, then makes a pullback down.

**Head,** price rises above the previous top, then rolls back down again.

**Right shoulde**r, the price rises, but cannot exceed the head level and falls again.

**Neckline,** connects the two lows between the shoulders and the head. It is a key level.

> Breakdown of the neckline is the main signal to enter a trade.

### 📊 How to trade Head and Shoulders?

📉 Standard (bearish) version — sell signal

When to enter?

We wait for the price to break the neckline downwards.

Go short on confirmation (or on retest of the line).

Where should I place my stop loss?

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLf-yQSbEAAW4Lf.jpg?name=large)

Behind the right shoulder or slightly above the head — depending on the aggressiveness of the strategy.

Where is take profit?

Measure the distance from the head to the neck → postpone down from the breakdown of the neck line.

* * *

✅ Example trade:

Left shoulder at 100, head at 110, right shoulder at 101.

Neck line at 95.

After the downside breakdown, we put takeout at 80 (110–95 = 15 → 95–15 = 80).

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLgAOUnbIAA8MPP.jpg?name=large)

**💡 How to improve accuracy**?

Look at the volumes: when the head is forming, the volume often decreases, but when the neckline is broken, it should increase sharply.

Confirm with other indicators: **Price Channel**,**RSI, MACD, Volume**— they will help to filter out false signals.

Watch for symmetry, the more “neat” the figure, the more reliable the signal.

> ❗ Pitfalls

> False breakouts: sometimes the price makes a “false shot” over the neckline and then comes back.

Misrecognition: sometimes a trader sees a pattern where it is not. It is better to wait for a clear breakout.

This pattern may work worse in a sideways trend — it is better to look for it on a pronounced trend.

“Head and Shoulders” is one of the most recognizable and reliable reversal patterns. The main thing is not to hurry, wait for the full formation and breakdown of the neckline, and then make a decision.

> trade on the trend before the signal, not on expectations. And never guess where the right shoulder will be, the market will show you everything.
