**What is it?**

“Cup and Handle is a trend continuation chart pattern. It shows that the market has rested after the growth, gathered its strength, and is now ready to continue the upward movement.

**Visually, the pattern looks like… an ordinary cup with a handle:**

A round or U-shaped depression is the cup.

A slight correction to the side is the handle.

_**In this article, I will explain in simple terms how the pattern works on Pacifica, how to use it correctly and what you should pay attention to**_**.**

![Image 1](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HL0mHZkWAAAg1_t.jpg?name=large)

**Where does it appear?**

Typically formed after an uptrend.

It looks like a long accumulation base, where the price “matures” to a new impulse.

**What does it look like on a chart?**

A rise to a certain level (say 100).

A pullback down (to, say, 90), then a smooth upward reversal back to 100, this is a “cup”.

After the re-approach to 100, a small pullback or sideways (95–97) — this is a “handle”.

Breakout of the 100 level up → buy signal.

![Image 2](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HL0us5BXEAAYGG3.jpg?name=large)

**How to trade on the “cup and handle” on Pacifica?**

1️⃣ Entry

Enter when the upper boundary of the cup (resistance level) is broken.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

![Image 3](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HL0vR0sWkAAyeC8.jpg?name=large)

You can enter on a pullback after the breakdown (retest of the handle) safer.

2️⃣ Stop loss

We put it under the minimum of the handle.

![Image 4](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HL0vbRKWUAA16Zo.png?name=large)

Or, if you risk less under the middle of the cup (deeper, but safer).

3️⃣ Take Profit

We measure the depth of the cup (from the resistance level to the bottom).

![Image 5](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HL0wWs0WoAAIHbk.jpg?name=large)

We postpone this distance upwards from the breakout point.

Resistance level: 100

Bottom of the cup: 90

Breakdown at 101

Target: 101 + (100–90) = 111

**🧠 How to recognize a “real” cup**?

The shape of the cup should be smooth, not V-shaped.

The handle is shallow in depth, at most 1/3 of the depth of the cup.

Volumes: the volume may decrease during cup formation, but at breakdown it should increase!

✅ Pros and ❌ Cons

✅ Advantages:

Gives a clear signal for continued growth.

It works on all timeframes: from days to minutes.

Psychologically explainable: the market has “digested” the growth and is ready for a new one.

**❌ Disadvantages:**

It takes a long time to form, requires patience.

May be a false breakout, especially without volumes.

Hard to spot on fluctuations that are too sharp.

The cup with a handle is one of the favorite patterns among traders. It predicts growth not on nothing, but after the market “rests.

If you see beautiful symmetry, breakout and volume growth is this is your chance to enter a strong move with good potential.

> **Tip:**works better in a strong bull market or on trending stocks, cryptocurrencies.

_**Combine with RSI, MACD or volumes for reliability.**_
