Weekly 10,000,000-point distribution, snapshots every Thursday, and what's tracked — and what isn't.

![Points program](../assets/img/points-program.png)

## TL;DR

* The Pacifica Points program **launched Thursday, September 4, 2025, at 00:00 UTC**.

* **10,000,000 points** distributed **weekly** to eligible users.

* **Snapshots every Thursday at 00:00 UTC**; distributions within 24 hours.

* The formula is **opaque by design** — no public weights, no farming trick.

## 19.1. The basics

The program is weekly. Every Thursday at 00:00 UTC a snapshot is taken, and within 24 hours the weekly allocation is distributed.[1]

* **Snapshot time:** every Thursday, 00:00 UTC.

* **Distribution window:** within 24 hours after snapshot.

* **Weekly pool:** 10,000,000 points.

## 19.2. What's tracked

The docs say many activities are tracked, and attribution may be immediate or applied over multiple distributions. The exact list is not published. What is clear from the FAQ:

* "Self-trading, sybil or any other manipulative activities will not earn points."

* "Discrete or costless actions do not yield rewards in isolation. Having 100 accounts provides no advantage over one."

* "Pacifica's points program is structured to balance both early supporters and long-term contributors fairly."

The implication: **points are an anti-gamed signal of organic usage**, not a public formula. The program is designed to reward real trading activity in a way that's hard to farm.

## 19.3. What counts

Many activities are tracked. The high-level categories:

* **Trading activity** — perpetual and spot volume, in a way that's resistant to self-trading.

* **Account longevity / consistency** — sustained activity over time is rewarded.

* **Ecosystem engagement** — providing feedback, spreading the word, governance participation.

Specific multipliers and weights are **not published**. The official FAQ says the formula "is dynamic and opaque by design, updated weekly as usage evolves."[1]

> "Very few Pacifica team members. Only Pacifica's community members are eligible to earn points in order to ensure fairness and prevent abuse."

## 19.4. The anti-gaming design

The two most important anti-gaming properties:

* **Self-trading doesn't count.** If you buy and sell against yourself to inflate volume, you earn nothing.

* **Multi-accounting doesn't help.** "Having 100 accounts provides no advantage over one" because discrete actions don't yield rewards in isolation — only organic usage does.

The implication for a would-be farmer: it's not worth the time. The opportunity cost of capital tied up in fake activity is higher than the expected reward.

## 19.5. Secondary markets

> "While Pacifica cannot/will not actively police this, we do not condone any such activity and strongly discourage this for security reasons."

The team won't go after secondary-market trading of points, but they don't want it. The reason is that secondary markets attract phishing, social engineering, and account-takeover attempts against naive users.

## 19.6. How to participate

* Trade on Pacifica via the GUI or API. Both count.

* Don't try to game the formula. The formula is opaque, but the anti-gaming is not.

* Use the program as a passive reward for activity you'd be doing anyway. The expected value is positive for any real trading volume; it's not worth trading more than you would just for points.

## 19.7. The points leaderboard

The points leaderboard is visible in the app at the **Points** entry point. It shows your current rank, recent distributions, and historical performance. The leaderboard is not a guaranteed outcome — the points are not redeemable in any announced way at the time of writing.

The implicit assumption is that **points will become relevant for a future token distribution, fee discount, or other program**. The team hasn't announced the exact mechanism.

## Pitfalls

* **Chasing the leaderboard.** The formula is opaque and designed to resist farming. Trading more than your strategy dictates in pursuit of points is usually net-negative.

* **Creating multiple accounts to multiply rewards.** The anti-gaming is designed for exactly this.

* **Buying points on a secondary market.** Pacifica discourages it for good reason; the points are tied to the issuing wallet.

* **Treating points as a tradable asset today.** They have no public utility yet.
