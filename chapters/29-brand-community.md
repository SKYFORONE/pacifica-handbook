How to use Pacifica's brand assets, the official channels, and how to talk to the team.

## TL;DR

* The official **brand assets** live in a public Google Drive folder; use them as-is, don't modify.

* The team is reachable via **Discord**, **Telegram**, **X**, and **email** (`ops@pacifica.fi`).

* The status page is at **status.pacifica.fi**; check it during incidents.

* The mobile app is on **Google Play** (Android) with package `com.pacifica.app`.

## 29.1. The brand assets

The official brand assets are hosted in a public Google Drive folder:[1]

> [Brand Assets Repository](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1cpktvx3Ir4OZVjEiyqY3VcY3Y87_PTnb?usp=sharing)

The folder includes high-resolution logos (light and dark) and vector files. The brand page in the docs notes that the assets exist to ensure "a consistent and professional presentation across all channels." If you're a partner, media outlet, or community member producing Pacifica-related content:

* Use the assets as provided.

* Don't modify the logos, color palette, or typography.

* Don't imply endorsement without explicit permission.

## 29.2. The visual style

From the live site, documentation, and app, the visual language is:

* **Geometric and clean.** Sharp lines, rounded corners, no decorative flourishes.

* **Dark-mode first.** The default app theme is dark; a light theme is available.

* **Mint-green accent.** A signature emerald (`#4ADE80` and `#117866` family) is the brand color.

* **Monospaced numerics.** Prices, sizes, and quantities are rendered in a monospaced face for readability.

* **Subtle gridlines.** Orderbook and chart backgrounds use faint grid patterns.

* **No celebrity faces, no stock photography.** The brand avoids lifestyle imagery; the visual content is product-first.

## 29.4. How to talk to the team

For different intents, different channels work best:

* **"I have a trading question"** → Discord #support channel, or Telegram.

* **"I have a bug or security finding"** → `ops@pacifica.fi` (flag as important/urgent) or Discord ticket. Test on testnet.

* **"I want to be a market maker"** → `ops@pacifica.fi` or Discord ticket.

* **"I want to be an affiliate"** → Discord ticket, with details about your community.

* **"I want to apply for VIP tier carry-over"** → Discord or Telegram with the @PacificaTGPortalBot.

* **"I have a partnership / press inquiry"** → `ops@pacifica.fi`.

* **"I want to build on Pacifica"** → `ops@pacifica.fi` or Discord (Builder Program).

The team explicitly notes: "We're here to help. Whether you have a question about trading, need technical support, or want to share your feedback, feel free to reach out to us through any of the channels."[2]

## 29.5. The status page

For live system status — engine, bridge, withdraw, deposit, all subsystems — go to **[status.pacifica.fi](https://status.pacifica.fi/)**.[3]

During an incident, the status page is the **single source of truth** for what's working. Social channels may have noise; the status page is the official record. Subscribe if you're running automated strategies.

## 29.6. The community culture

The Pacifica community skews toward:

* **Active traders.** Many members are full-time perps traders looking for a CEX-grade experience with self-custody.

* **AI-curious builders.** The MCP and AI Agent are first-class features; the community is experimenting with agents.

* **HFT / market-making teams.** Several professional market-making groups are present and active in the Discord.

* **Vault operators.** A growing number of users run vaults; LPs follow them.

The community is global and English-first, with active presences on Discord, X, and Telegram.

## 29.7. The content production etiquette

If you produce Pacifica content — videos, articles, tutorials, courses — keep these in mind:

* **Verify the version.** Pacifica is under active development. Parameters and limits can change. Date your content.

* **Cite the docs.** Link to the relevant docs page; don't paraphrase specs you don't fully understand.

* **Show your work.** Worked examples with the actual numbers from the docs are more credible than screenshots.

* **Disclose your wallet.** If you use a referral code, the community expects transparency.

* **Don't shill.** Educational content ages better than promotional content.

## Pitfalls

* **Using outdated screenshots.** The UI evolves; a screenshot from six months ago is misleading.

* **Trusting unofficial Telegram groups.** The only official Telegram is the @PacificaTGPortalBot. Anything else is a scam.

* **DMing random team members for support.** Use the official channels; the team is more responsive there.

* **Treating the status page as optional.** Bookmark it; check it before assuming the platform is broken.
